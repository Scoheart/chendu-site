Rising oil prices, falling technology stocks and reversing bond yields keep Wall Street unsettled

油价飙升、科技股回调、国债收益率掉头：华尔街又是心神不宁的一天

By Stan Choe · The Associated Press · 2026-10-08 16:41 EDT（北京时间 10 月 9 日 04:41）

今天读美联社（AP）的收盘综述。这类“每日股市报道”句式高度模板化，学会一篇，以后读 Reuters、CNBC 都轻松很多。重点看：涨跌动词怎么换着用、长句里的让步与原因结构。

NEW YORK (AP) — Some sharp reversals1 kept financial markets unsteady2 on Thursday, as oil prices rose and U.S. stock indexes finished mixed3.

1词汇
sharp reversal
急剧反转、大逆转。reverse 的名词。sharp 在财经新闻里修饰变化 = “剧烈的、陡的”：a sharp drop 暴跌，a sharp rise 急涨。
3地道表达
finish mixed
收盘涨跌互现。股市报道固定说法：finish/close higher 收涨，finish lower 收跌，finish mixed 有涨有跌。
2语法
keep + 宾语 + 形容词
keep sb/sth adj. = 使……保持某种状态。主语是 reversals（事），宾语是 markets，unsteady 是宾补。比 “made markets unsteady” 多了“持续”的意味。

The S&P 500 fell 0.5% for a second straight loss4 after setting its all-time high5. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 51 points, or 0.1%, and the Nasdaq composite fell 1.3% as technology stocks took particularly hard hits6.

5词汇
all-time high
历史最高点（反义 all-time low）。set / hit an all-time high 创历史新高。也可说 a record high。
4地道表达
a second straight loss
连续第二天下跌。straight 这里不是“直的”，而是“连续的”= consecutive。常见：for the third straight week 连续第三周。
6地道表达
take a hit
遭受打击、受损。take a hard hit = 受重创。生活中也常用：My savings took a hit this month.（这个月我的存款缩水了。）

Stocks felt pressure from a 4.1% rise in the price for a barrel of Brent crude oil, the international standard7, to $104.28. It’s been pinballing8 between $96 and nearly $110 over the last month on uncertainty about when the war with Iran will allow the global energy industry to return to normal9.

7语法
同位语
逗号中间的 the international standard 是同位语，补充说明 Brent crude（布伦特原油）= 国际基准油价。新闻里大量用 “名词, 解释性名词短语,” 来省掉一个从句。
9语法
长难句拆解
on + 名词 = 因为……（on fears/hopes/news of… 财经高频）。about 后面跟 when 引导的名词性从句；从句内部是 allow sth to do（让……得以做）。直译：因为不确定与伊朗的战争何时能让全球能源业恢复正常。
8地道表达
pinball (v.)
pinball 本是“弹珠台”，这里活用为动词：像弹珠一样来回乱弹 → 价格剧烈来回波动。It’s been pinballing 是现在完成进行时，强调“过去一个月一直在”。
[ … ]

On Wall Street, the ease in Treasury yields10 helped the majority of U.S. stocks rise, including two out of every three11 in the S&P 500 index.

11地道表达
two out of every three
每三只里就有两只。表示比例的地道说法：one in five / one out of every five people。
10词汇
ease / Treasury yields
ease 名词 = 缓和、回落（动词：yields eased）。Treasury yields 美国国债收益率；收益率回落通常利好股市。

PepsiCo climbed 3.7% after the snack and drink company reported stronger profit and revenue for the latest quarter than analysts expected12, thanks in part to13 strength outside of North America.

13地道表达
thanks in part to
部分得益于……。thanks to 本身就是“由于”（多用于好结果），插入 in part 让语气更严谨：不是全部原因，而是原因之一。
12语法
比较结构被拉长
stronger … than analysts expected：stronger 和 than 中间夹了 “profit and revenue for the latest quarter”，读的时候要把两头连起来。= beat analysts’ expectations 超出分析师预期。profit 利润，revenue 营收。

But drops for several influential technology stocks overshadowed14 such gains.

14词汇
overshadow
使相形见绌、盖过。over + shadow：影子盖在上面。科技股的跌幅“盖过”了其他股票的涨幅，所以指数整体还是跌。

Nvidia, the chip company that’s ridden the tidal wave of demand15 created by artificial-intelligence technology, fell 2.9%. Because it’s the largest stock by value16 on Wall Street, it was the heaviest weight on the S&P 500 even though other stocks had larger losses.

15语法
that’s = that has
注意这里 ’s 是 has 不是 is！that has ridden（ride-rode-ridden）现在完成时。ride the wave 乘势而上，tidal wave 巨浪 → 乘着 AI 需求的巨浪一路高歌。
16词汇
by value
按市值算最大（by market value / market cap）。由于指数按市值加权，英伟达跌得不算最多，却是拖累 S&P 500 最重的一只：the heaviest weight on…

That included drops for other AI-related stocks, including Broadcom’s 4.3% fall and Micron Technology’s 4.8% slide17.

17语法
名词化表达
不写 “Micron fell 4.8%”，而写 Micron’s 4.8% slide：所有格 + 幅度 + 名词。动词变名词，信息更紧凑，是新闻英语典型写法。slide 下滑，同类词：drop, fall, decline, dip, slump。

The losses came even though18 a bellwether19 for the chip industry, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co., reported growth for September that suggested its revenue for the latest quarter was strong enough to top analysts’ expectations20. TSMC’s stock that trades in the United States fell 3%.

19词汇
bellwether
风向标、领头羊。原意是“脖子上挂铃铛的领头公羊”。a bellwether for the industry = 行业风向标。
18语法
长难句：主干 + 让步
主干：The losses came（下跌出现了）。even though 引导让步：主语 a bellwether…, TSMC,（同位语）→ 谓语 reported growth → that suggested… 修饰 growth → (that) its revenue was strong enough to top… 是 suggested 的宾语从句。
20地道表达
top expectations
top 作动词 = 超过。top / beat / exceed expectations 超预期；反义 miss expectations / fall short of expectations。

AI stocks are under heavy pressure to report big growth to justify21 how high their stock prices have soared in recent years because of the AI frenzy22.

21语法
两个不定式连用
be under pressure to do（被迫要做……）+ to justify（目的：为了证明……合理）。justify 的宾语是 how high their stock prices have soared（how + 形容词 引导宾语从句）。
22词汇
frenzy
狂热、疯狂。the AI frenzy = AI 热潮（带一点“不理性”的意味）。近义：craze, mania, hype。

可以模仿的句型

  1. [事件] kept [某对象] + adj. 某事使……一直处于某种状态
    原文：Some sharp reversals kept financial markets unsteady on Thursday.
    The surprise rate hike kept investors nervous all week.
    意外加息让投资者整整一周都提心吊胆。
  2. A happened, thanks in part to B. A 发生了，部分得益于 B
    原文：PepsiCo climbed 3.7% … thanks in part to strength outside of North America.
    My English improved a lot this year, thanks in part to reading the news every morning.
    今年我的英语进步很大，部分要归功于每天早上读新闻。
  3. The [结果] came even though [让步]. 尽管……，（结果）还是出现了
    原文：The losses came even though a bellwether for the chip industry … reported growth.
    The sell-off came even though the company beat earnings expectations.
    尽管公司财报超预期，抛售还是来了。
  4. be under (heavy) pressure to do sth to justify sth 面临（巨大）压力要做……来证明……合理
    原文：AI stocks are under heavy pressure to report big growth to justify how high their stock prices have soared.
    Startups are under pressure to show profits to justify their valuations.
    创业公司面临压力，要拿出利润来证明估值合理。
