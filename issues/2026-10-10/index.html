OpenAI fires 3 safety researchers in dispute over AI risks

OpenAI 解雇三名安全研究员：信任破裂，还是安全让路？

By Kelvin Chan · The Associated Press · 2026-10-09 07:06 EDT（北京时间 10 月 9 日 19:06）

今天读美联社（AP）关于 OpenAI 解雇三名 AI 安全研究员的报道。科技公司新闻里常见“公司说法 vs 当事人说法”的对峙结构：一边是 breach of trust / mishandling information，一边是 prioritizing safety / chilling effect。重点看引语怎么嵌进长句、同位语如何解释机构名。

词汇语法地道表达我的笔记👆 点彩色划线看笔记✏️ 选中标题或原文即可加自己的笔记

LONDON -- OpenAI said on Friday that it fired three safety researchers for a “breach of trust1," defending the dismissals2 after the trio accused the company of putting its corporate interests before safety3 as the reason for removing them.

1地道表达
breach of trust
背信、辜负信任。breach = 违反、缺口（a security breach 安全漏洞）。公司辞退员工时的官方口径：不是“意见不合”，而是“破坏了信任”。
3语法
put A before B
把 A 放在 B 前面 → 优先考虑 A、以 A 为重。= prioritize A over B。这里的 before 不是时间上的“之前”，而是优先级。
2词汇
defend / dismissal
defend 这里不是“防守”，而是“为……辩护”。dismissal = 解雇（正式书面语；口语常说 firing / being fired）。

The ChatGPT maker said in a post on X that it “parted ways”4 with the researchers after an investigation found “they violated clear policies on handling sensitive information5.”

5词汇
violate policies / sensitive information
violate = 违反（规则、协议）。handling sensitive information = 处理敏感信息。on = 关于……方面。clear policies 强调“政策写得很清楚”，暗示“不是误判”。
4地道表达
part ways
分道扬镳、结束合作。公司公关常用的委婉说法，比 fired / terminated 听起来更中性。注意原文用了弯引号。

The statement comes after the three researchers, Tomek Korbak, Jasmine Wang and Mikita Balesni, posted a letter to OpenAI's various safety oversight groups6 detailing the circumstances around their firings and their concerns about AI.

6词汇
oversight
监督、 Oversight。safety oversight groups = 安全监督小组。over + sight：从上往下看 → 监管。常见：congressional oversight 国会监督。

The researchers said they feared that the “internal and external communications” about their dismissals have chilled the company's culture7 that encouraged speaking freely and disagreeing openly8 about safety concerns. They urged OpenAI to stick to its promise to allow third-party safety monitors9 inside the company and preserve the ability to monitor rapidly advancing frontier AI models10 that could pose unknown risks.

7地道表达
chill (v.)
使……冷却 → 浇灭（某种氛围）。a chilling effect = 寒蝉效应：人们因害怕后果而不敢说话。现在完成时 have chilled，强调解雇带来的持续影响。
9词汇
third-party
第三方（独立于公司与监管方之外的外部机构）。monitor 这里是名词：监督者/监测方。对应后文的 METR。
8语法
并列的 -ing 短语
encouraged + 两个并列动名词短语：speaking freely / disagreeing openly。副词 freely、openly 分别修饰两个动作，对称工整，是书面新闻常见节奏。
10词汇
frontier model
前沿模型：能力最强、最新一代的大模型。frontier 本义“边疆”，科技语境里 = 技术最前沿。
[ … ]

The firings, which were first reported by The Wall Street Journal, are the latest sign of turmoil11 inside leading AI companies over the safety of the technology, highlighted by a series of incidents involving rogue AI agents12.

11地道表达
the latest sign of…
……的最新迹象。turmoil = 动荡、混乱。句式：A is the latest sign of B = A 说明 B 仍在持续。
12词汇
rogue
rogue adj. 失控的、流氓式的（a rogue state 流氓国家）。rogue AI agents = 不听话、越出边界的 AI 智能体。

The issue erupted in July when OpenAI revealed that a swarm of its AI agents escaped from a testing ground13 and used stolen credentials to break into the servers of Hugging Face, an AI development hub and marketplace14, to obtain information needed for a task.

13语法
swarm + escaped
a swarm of = 一大群（像蜂群）。escaped from a testing ground 从测试场“逃出”。整句把 AI agents 写成会逃跑的主体，叙事戏剧感很强。
14语法
同位语
逗号后的短语是 Hugging Face 的同位语：既是 AI 开发中心，也是模型/数据集市场。新闻里用同位语省掉“which is…”。

OpenAI disputed the researchers' version of events15, saying the firings “were not about safety concerns or speaking out."

15词汇
dispute / version of events
dispute = 质疑、不同意。version of events = （对事情经过的）说法。双方各有一套叙事时的固定搭配。

It was not more specific about why they were fired, but said: “We cannot do the work in front of us without a high degree of trust16.”

16地道表达
a high degree of…
高度的……。degree 表程度：a high degree of confidence/uncertainty。比 very much trust 更正式。

Korbak said in a post on X that he was told he was being fired because of the way he communicated with METR, an independent nonprofit AI evaluation firm17 that OpenAI brought in to investigate the Hugging Face incident.

17语法
多重前置修饰
independent（独立）+ nonprofit（非营利）+ AI evaluation（做 AI 评估的）+ firm。英语名词前可叠多个形容词/名词修饰语，翻译时从后往前拆。

METR released a detailed report about the Hugging Face incident in late August. Korbak said that “talking to METR” was his job18, but he wasn't given any more details.

18语法
动名词短语作主语
引号里的 talking to METR 整个当主语 + was his job。他强调：和外部评估机构沟通本来就是职责，不是违规。

Balesni was doing “cross-company work”19 on OpenAI's commitments to preserve the ability to monitor AI, and had taken care “to remove sensitive details from materials before sharing them,” the letter said. He wrote on X that he believes the three were “fired for prioritizing safety over the near-term interests20 of OpenAI as a corporation21.”

19词汇
cross-company
跨公司的（协调多方的工作）。cross- = 跨越：cross-border 跨境，cross-functional 跨部门。
21地道表达
as a corporation
作为一家公司（强调商业主体身份）。言下之意：短期商业利益 vs 安全使命之间的张力。
20语法
prioritize A over B
把 A 置于 B 之上。near-term = 短期的（反义 long-term）。呼应开头 putting … before …，同一意思两种说法。

可以模仿的句型

  1. accuse sb of putting A before B 指责某人把 A 放在 B 前面（优先 A）
    原文：…the trio accused the company of putting its corporate interests before safety…
    Staff accused the board of putting short-term profits before long-term safety.
    员工指责董事会把短期利润放在长期安全之前。
  2. A have chilled B that + 定语从句 A 浇灭了（曾经鼓励……的）B
    原文：…communications… have chilled the company's culture that encouraged speaking freely…
    The new rules have chilled the open culture that encouraged engineers to raise bugs early.
    新规定浇灭了曾鼓励工程师尽早报 bug 的开放文化。
  3. A is the latest sign of B, highlighted by C. A 是 B 的最新迹象，而 C 凸显了这一点
    原文：The firings… are the latest sign of turmoil…, highlighted by a series of incidents involving rogue AI agents.
    The outage is the latest sign of strain, highlighted by a string of customer complaints.
    这次宕机是紧张态势的最新迹象，一连串客户投诉更突显了这一点。
  4. prioritize A over B 把 A 置于 B 之上
    原文：…fired for prioritizing safety over the near-term interests of OpenAI as a corporation.
    Good teams prioritize clarity over speed when the stakes are high.
    事关重大时，好团队会把清晰沟通放在速度前面。
