US stocks drop to their worst day since July as inflation worsens

通胀恶化拖累美股：标普创 7 月来最差单日，AI 股领跌

By Stan Choe · The Associated Press · 2026-10-14 16:03 EDT（北京时间 10 月 15 日 04:03；WRAL 页显示更新至 20:48 EDT）

今天读美联社（AP）周三收盘综述。9 月 CPI 升至 3.7%，超预期，美债收益率跳升，标普跌 1.1%、纳指跌 1.9%，Nvidia / Broadcom / AMD / CoreWeave 等 AI 相关股领跌。重点看：worst day since / rising yields / rein in / ride the AI frenzy / heaviest weights 这类财经套话。

U.S. stocks dropped to their worst day1 since July on Wednesday following the latest signal2 that inflation is getting worse3.

1地道表达
drop to one’s worst day
跌至……以来最差的一天。worst day since July = 7 月以来最差单日。新闻收盘常用。
3词汇
get worse
变得更糟。get + adj. 表变化：get worse / get better。标题也用 worsen。
2语法
following + n.
在……之后；随着……。following the latest signal = 在最新信号出现之后。比 after 更书面。

The S&P 500 sank 1.1% for a fifth loss4 in the six days since setting its all-time high5. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 48 points, or 0.1%, and the Nasdaq composite slid 1.9% as stocks in the artificial-intelligence industry led the way lower6.

5词汇
all-time high
历史新高。set / hit an all-time high。对 all-time low。
4语法
for a …th …
这是第……次……。for a fifth loss = 这是第五次下跌。for a second straight day = 连续第二天。
6地道表达
lead the way
带头……。led the way lower = 领跌。也可 lead the way higher = 领涨。

Stocks felt pressure from7 rising yields8 in the bond market, where worries grew after a report said U.S. consumers had to pay prices for fuel, food and other costs of living9 last month that were 3.7% higher overall than a year earlier. That means inflation accelerated10 from its 3.4% rate in August and was worse than the 3.6% that economists expected.

7地道表达
feel pressure from
感受到来自……的压力。Stocks felt pressure from rising yields = 股指因债市收益率上行承压。
9词汇
cost of living
生活成本。costs of living = 衣食住行等日常开销。CPI 衡量的就是这方面价格变化。
8词汇
yield
（债券）收益率。rising yields = 收益率上行，通常压制股价。10-year Treasury yield = 十年期美债收益率。
10词汇
accelerate
加速。inflation accelerated = 通胀加快。反义 decelerate / ease。

Inflation has remained more painful than anyone would like since the economy catapulted out of11 the crevasse12 created by the COVID-19 pandemic. It’s accelerated recently13 in part because of jumps in fuel prices14 caused by the war with Iran, and the price for a barrel of Brent crude added 0.4% Wednesday to $106.06.

11词汇
catapult
弹射、猛然推出。catapulted out of the crevasse = 从深渊里猛冲出来。形象写疫情后经济反弹。
13语法
It’s = It has
这里 It’s = It has（现在完成时），不是 It is。看后面 accelerated（过去分词）可判断。
12词汇
crevasse
（冰川）裂缝、深渊。这里比喻疫情造成的经济深坑。比 hole / gap 更有冲击力。
14地道表达
a jump in
……的跳升。jumps in fuel prices = 燃料价格的几次急涨。财经常用 a jump / surge / spike in。

The Fed recently raised the interest rate that it controls, which affects very short-term loans, for the first time in three years in hopes of reining in inflation15. Higher rates do that by making it more expensive16 for everyone to borrow money and slowing the overall economy.

15地道表达
rein in
勒住、遏制。rein in inflation = 把通胀勒住。本义：收紧缰绳。
16语法
make it + adj. + to do
使做某事更……。making it more expensive … to borrow = 让借钱更贵。it 是形式宾语。

Traders now see a nearly 35% chance the Fed will raise the federal funds rate17 at its next meeting in two weeks, up from the 22% probability18 seen a day earlier, according to data from CME Group.

17词汇
federal funds rate
联邦基金利率。美联储设定的隔夜拆借目标利率，加息/降息说的就是它。
18语法
up from
较……上升。up from 22% = 从 22% 升上来。对比：down from / versus。
[ … ]

Much of Wall Street’s pain19 on Wednesday was due to drops for companies riding the AI frenzy20 to huge profit growth and stellar stock gains21.

19地道表达
pain
这里 pain = 下跌之痛、市场难受。Wall Street’s pain = 华尔街承压/受伤。注意 Street’s 用弯引号。
21词汇
stellar
杰出的、星级的。stellar gains / performance = 亮眼涨幅/表现。
20地道表达
ride the … frenzy
搭上……狂热的顺风车。riding the AI frenzy = 借 AI 狂热行情。frenzy = 狂热。

Higher bond yields making borrowing money more expensive22 could make it more difficult for companies to raise cash23 to build data centers and fill them with AI chips. Drops of 3.2% for Nvidia, 3.1% for Broadcom and 5.7% for Advanced Micro Devices were among the day’s heaviest weights on24 the S&P 500.

23地道表达
raise cash / capital
筹资、融资。raise cash to build data centers = 筹钱建数据中心。
22语法
现在分词作定语/原因
Higher bond yields making … expensive = 收益率上行「使得」借钱更贵。分词短语修饰前句，表因果。
24地道表达
a heavy weight on
对指数拖累最大的成分。heaviest weights on the S&P 500 = 对标普拖累最重的几只股。

CoreWeave, which offers AI computing power to customers over the cloud, tumbled 15.5%25 for its worst day since February. GE Vernova, which is hoping to electrify power-hungry data centers26, dropped 6.2%, and Oracle sank 8.8%27.

25词汇
tumble
暴跌、急跌。tumble 15.5% = 暴跌 15.5%。比 fall / drop 更猛。
27词汇
sink
下沉、大幅下跌。sank 8.8% = 跌了 8.8%。财经常用 sink / slide / slip。
26词汇
power-hungry
极度耗电的。power-hungry = 吃电的、耗电大户。hunger for power。

All told,28 the S&P 500 fell 86.51 points to 7,718.94. The Dow Jones Industrial Average dipped 48.6229 to 51,621.48, and the Nasdaq composite sank 511.51 to 26,692.87.

29词汇
dip
小幅回落。dip 比 drop / tumble 轻：道指只 dip 了约 49 点（0.1%）。
28地道表达
all told
总的来说、合计。收盘综述常用 All told, the S&P 500 fell… = 全天合计……。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A dropped to their worst day since B following C 在 C 之后，A 跌至自 B 以来最差的一天
    原文：U.S. stocks dropped to their worst day since July on Wednesday following the latest signal that inflation is getting worse.
    Chip stocks dropped to their worst day since March following the weak guidance.
    在疲软展望公布后，芯片股跌至自 3 月以来最差的一天。
  2. A felt pressure from B, where C A 感受到来自 B 的压力，在那里 C
    原文：Stocks felt pressure from rising yields in the bond market, where worries grew after a report said…
    Banks felt pressure from rising defaults, where worries grew after the jobs report.
    银行感受到违约上升的压力，就业报告公布后担忧加剧。
  3. A raised B in hopes of reining in C A 上调 B，以期遏制 C
    原文：The Fed recently raised the interest rate that it controls… in hopes of reining in inflation.
    The company raised prices in hopes of reining in losses.
    公司提价以期遏制亏损。
  4. Drops of X for A … were among the day’s heaviest weights on B A 下跌 X……是当日对 B 拖累最重的之一
    原文：Drops of 3.2% for Nvidia, 3.1% for Broadcom and 5.7% for Advanced Micro Devices were among the day’s heaviest weights on the S&P 500.
    Drops of 4% for Meta were among the day’s heaviest weights on the Nasdaq.
    Meta 下跌 4% 是当日对纳指拖累最重的之一。
