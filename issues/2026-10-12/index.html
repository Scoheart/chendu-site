In Silicon Valley, AI founders cheer Trump’s calls for the industry to police itself on safety

硅谷为“自我监管”欢呼：白宫放手，实验室自查？

By Garance Burke · The Associated Press · 2026-10-11 03:08 EDT（北京时间 10 月 11 日 15:08）

今天读美联社（AP）从旧金山 Tech Week 发回的报道。一边是创投对 hands-off 监管的 ebullient，一边是 OpenAI 前安全负责人警告 self-policing 代替不了 hard law。重点看：ebullient / hands-off / stymie / unbridled / stake political capital 这类政策与科技新闻套话。

SAN FRANCISCO (AP) — Tech Week is in full bloom1 in San Francisco, packing the city's restaurants this week with venture capitalists and AI founders ebullient about2 the White House's stance on regulating artificial intelligence — namely, don't do it3.

1地道表达
in full bloom
盛开、正热闹。本义花开正盛，这里喻 Tech Week 活动开到最旺。也可说 the festival is in full bloom。
3语法
namely
也就是、即。后面直接点明前文 stance 的具体内容：don't do it = 别监管。冒号或破折号后也常见。
2词汇
ebullient
热情洋溢的、兴高采烈的。ebullient about X = 对 X 兴奋得不行。比 excited / upbeat 更书面、更“冒泡”。

And after months of debate about leading AI companies' unchecked AI agents4 meddling with5 everything from U.S. government websites to healthcare data, the nation now finds itself further from governing AI systems than it was a few years ago.

5词汇
meddle with
插手、乱碰。带负面色彩：agents meddling with government websites = 擅自介入。名词 meddling。
4词汇
unchecked
不受约束的、未加遏制的。unchecked growth / power / agents。和上一期 rogue agents 同一话题线。

President Donald Trump brought tech titans6 to Washington late last month for a luncheon and ceremonial signing of a voluntary safety pact7 he described as "almost a constitution" surrounding "Super Intelligence," the administration's new preferred term for AI.

7词汇
pact
协定、契约。voluntary = 自愿的（非强制立法）。和 hard law 形成对照，是全文核心张力。
6地道表达
titan
巨头。tech titans = 科技界大佬。财经新闻固定说法，也说 industry titans。

The 308-word agreement lays out that8 advanced AI labs should follow safety protocols and set up internal teams to monitor their own progress, but the administration has yet to provide details on how, in OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's words, to keep "bad things" from happening.

8语法
lay out that…
阐明、规定……。协议/计划 lays out that… = 写明要求。也可 lay out a plan / the rules。

Safety challenges were not top of mind9 inside a crowded event space in San Francisco on Wednesday, where founders and funders said the government's hands-off approach10 would net higher profits11 for companies building AI-powered defense and security systems.

9地道表达
top of mind
最先想到的、当下最关心的。not top of mind = 根本没放在心上。营销/政治新闻高频。
11词汇
net (v.)
净得、带来（利润）。net higher profits = 净赚更多。名词 net = 净值/球网。
10地道表达
hands-off
不干预的、放手的。hands-off approach / policy = 少管、不管。反义 hands-on。
[ … ]

Panelists praised the government's bullish industry support12 at one meeting aimed at "investing in the forefront," part of the blitz of popup events13 organized by Andreessen Horowitz, a venture firm whose head is a Trump science and tech adviser.

13地道表达
blitz
闪电战式密集活动。a blitz of events = 一连串快闪活动。popup = 快闪、临时弹出的。
12词汇
bullish
看涨的、乐观支持的。来自股市 bull market。bullish on AI / bullish support = 力挺、看好。

A day later, three researchers at OpenAI's headquarters said the ChatGPT maker had fired them, posting that "fear and unclear rules stymie AI safety work14." Separately, David Robinson, a leader on OpenAI's safety team until he recently resigned, said he was worried about letting the advanced labs police themselves15.

15地道表达
police oneself
自我监管。let labs police themselves = 让实验室自己管自己。全文标题里的 police itself 同源。
14词汇
stymie
阻碍、卡住。fear and unclear rules stymie… = 恐惧与规则不清拖累安全工作。比 block 更书面。

"This is no substitute for hard law16, and it does give a lot of freedom to the companies," Robinson said. "The time between model generations is measured in weeks17 … and I think we have some very serious safety challenges in front of us in the nearer term."

17语法
be measured in…
以……为单位来衡量。time… is measured in weeks = 间隔短到论周。强调迭代极快。
16地道表达
no substitute for
代替不了……。hard law = 硬法（有强制力的立法），对 soft law / voluntary pact。

Trump has staked his political capital18 on the AI industry's success, saying its unbridled development19 is key to winning the AI race against China. On the first day of his second term, the Republican president repealed a Biden-era executive order20 aimed in part at guarding against the risks that AI systems pose to consumers, workers and national security.

19词汇
unbridled
不受缰绳约束的、放纵的。unbridled growth / ambition。和 unchecked 近义，画面感更强。
18地道表达
stake political capital
押上政治资本。stake A on B = 把 A 押在 B 上。政治新闻高频：赌声誉、押注成功。
20词汇
repeal
废止（法律/行政令）。executive order = 行政命令。Biden-era = 拜登时期的。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A are ebullient about B — namely, C A 对 B 热情高涨——也就是 C
    原文：…AI founders ebullient about the White House's stance on regulating artificial intelligence — namely, don't do it.
    Investors were ebullient about the new rules — namely, fewer limits on buybacks.
    投资者对新规热情高涨——也就是回购限制更少。
  2. A finds itself further from B than it was… A 发现自己比……时离 B 更远了
    原文：…the nation now finds itself further from governing AI systems than it was a few years ago.
    The team finds itself further from a product launch than it was last spring.
    团队发现自己比去年春天离产品发布更远了。
  3. This is no substitute for A 这代替不了 A
    原文：This is no substitute for hard law…
    A voluntary checklist is no substitute for independent audits.
    自愿清单代替不了独立审计。
  4. sb has staked … capital on A, saying B 某人把……资本押在 A 上，并称 B
    原文：Trump has staked his political capital on the AI industry's success, saying its unbridled development is key to…
    The CEO has staked her reputation on the new chip, saying speed is key to winning customers.
    这位 CEO 把声誉押在新芯片上，并称速度是赢下客户的关键。
