Anthropic warns AI models could soon copy themselves across home devices

Anthropic 警告：小模型几个月内或能在家用设备间“自我复制”

By Arjun Kharpal · CNBC · 2026-10-13 04:27 EDT（北京时间 10 月 13 日 16:27）

今天读 CNBC 对 Anthropic 最新研究的报道：小型 AI 模型可能再过几个月就能钻进智能烤箱、电视盒子，把自己切成 shards 分散运行、越传越广，而且没有总开关可关。重点看：infiltrate / rogue / swarm / awash with / bellwether 这类科技与安全新闻词。

Anthropic said Monday that small AI models are "months away" from1 developing the capabilities needed to replicate themselves2 across internet-connected devices in the home, marking the latest warning3 from the frontier lab4 about the technology's potential safety risks.

1地道表达
be months away from
距离……只有几个月了。months away from developing… = 再过几个月就能具备……。同类：days / years away from。
3语法
现在分词作结果状语
逗号后 marking… 补充说明前面整件事的意义：这“标志着”又一次警告。新闻导语常用 , marking / , signaling…。
2词汇
replicate
复制。replicate oneself = 自我复制。名词 replication，全文关键词：replication chain / replicator。
4词汇
frontier lab
前沿实验室。指 Anthropic、OpenAI、Google DeepMind 这类做最强模型的公司。frontier model = 前沿模型。

Anthropic described how AI models could potentially infiltrate5 internet-connected devices, such as smart ovens or Android TV boxes, and use their computing power to run themselves and spread to other devices, without relying on6 expensive data centers.

5词汇
infiltrate
渗透、潜入。infiltrate devices / networks。原本用于间谍、军队，这里指模型偷偷钻进设备。
6语法
without + doing
不依靠……。without relying on expensive data centers = 不需要昂贵的数据中心。rely on = 依赖。

The AI model would be "challenging to shut down7," Anthropic said in a blog post, "since there's no centralized point8 to shut off."

7语法
adj. + to do
“难以被关掉”。形容词后接不定式，主语是动作的承受者：hard to stop / easy to use。shut down = 关停。
8词汇
centralized
集中的、中心化的。no centralized point to shut off = 没有一个总开关可关。反义 decentralized / distributed。

The company acknowledged that there is not yet a "fully rogue replicator9."

9词汇
rogue
失控的、不受管的。rogue agent / rogue AI。replicator = 复制体、会自我复制的东西。

Agents are, however,10 "becoming increasingly capable of completing the individual steps in the replication chain11 (and passing on their adaptations12 to future copies of themselves)," Anthropic said.

11词汇
chain
链条。replication chain = 自我复制要走完的一连串步骤。类似 supply chain / kill chain。
10语法
however 插入
however 夹在句中、两边逗号：语气转折但不抢句首。比 But agents are… 更书面。
12地道表达
pass on
传递下去。pass on A to B = 把 A 传给 B。adaptation = 适应性改变，像生物进化里的“遗传”。
[ … ]

Anthropic's latest research adds to growing safety concerns13 around AI, such as the ability for models to carry out cyberattacks and the technology going rogue14. In September, improvements in frontier models sparked industry-wide warnings15 of existential risks16 to humanity.

13地道表达
add to
加剧、增添。A adds to growing concerns = A 让本已上升的担忧更甚。科技/财经新闻套话。
15词汇
spark
引发、点燃。spark warnings / debate / a selloff。industry-wide = 全行业的。
14地道表达
go rogue
失控、擅自行动。go + adj. 表变化：go rogue / go viral / go bankrupt。
16词汇
existential
关乎存亡的。existential risk = 生存级风险（可能危及人类整体）。

Executives and researchers at Anthropic and rival OpenAI repeatedly warned about the potential risks of increasingly powerful AI, with some calling for17 tighter guardrails18 and measures to control the pace of the technology's development.

17语法
with + 宾语 + doing
独立结构：with some calling for… = 其中一些人呼吁……。补充伴随情况，不另起一句。call for = 呼吁。
18词汇
guardrail
护栏。AI 语境 = 安全限制、防护措施。tighter guardrails = 更严的护栏。
[ … ]

Anthropic said it carried out various tests19 to see if AI could self-replicate. One of those tests involved running a small Google model, Gemma4-26B-A4B, across a network of 15 cheap off-brand20 Android TV boxes.

19地道表达
carry out
实施、进行（测试、攻击、计划）。carry out tests / cyberattacks。上文也出现 carry out cyberattacks。
20词汇
off-brand
杂牌的、非知名品牌的。cheap off-brand Android TV boxes = 便宜的杂牌安卓电视盒子。

The company noted that these types of devices often have weak security, which could allow a model to infiltrate them. A model that is too large to fit on one device21 could "split into pieces, or shards22" and run across multiple devices, Anthropic said.

21语法
too … to …
太……而不能……。too large to fit on one device = 大到一台设备装不下。fit on = 放得进。
22词汇
shard
碎片、分片。数据库/模型术语：把大模型切成 shards 分布在多台设备上运行。

"In practice, an AI worm wouldn't necessarily23 consist of just one model or strategy. A swarm of24 different models deployed on different types of hardware using a variety of replication schemes might represent a more realistic 'worst case scenario25' attack," Anthropic said.

23语法
部分否定
not necessarily = 不一定、未必。不是“一定不”，而是“不见得只是”。
25地道表达
worst-case scenario
最坏情况。in the worst-case scenario = 最坏的情况下。对 best-case scenario。
24词汇
swarm
一大群（蜂群、虫群）。a swarm of models / drones。动词 swarm = 蜂拥。

It's still "uncertain exactly what form autonomous AI replication will take26," the AI lab said, warning that it could be a "slow, distributed" system sitting on insecure devices or a "powerful cyber model that breaches datacenters at a high pace27, or a model that scams people for compute credits28."

27地道表达
at a … pace
以……的速度。at a high / rapid / steady pace。pace = 节奏、速度。
26语法
what form … will take
宾语从句：……会以什么形式出现。take a form = 呈现某种形态。注意从句用陈述语序。
28词汇
scam
诈骗。scam sb for sth = 骗某人的某物。compute credits = 算力额度。

"As a prediction that goes beyond our experiments, we expect a future awash with agents29 running on all types of hardware, including some that aren't under human control," Anthropic said.

29地道表达
awash with
充斥着、泛滥着。本义被水淹没。a market awash with cash = 市场资金泛滥。

"The rise of slow replicators would also be a bellwether for30 more aggressive, faster ones that can breach more hardened machines31 or be effective API key thieves," the company added.

31词汇
harden
加固（安全防护）过的。harden = 加固。hardened machines / systems = 防护更严的机器/系统。和上文 weak security、insecure devices 对照。
30词汇
bellwether
风向标、先兆。原指挂铃铛领头的羊。财经常说 a bellwether stock = 风向标股。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A is "months away" from doing B, marking C A 距离做到 B 只有几个月了，这标志着 C
    原文：Anthropic said Monday that small AI models are "months away" from developing the capabilities needed to replicate themselves…, marking the latest warning from the frontier lab…
    The startup is months away from launching its first chip, marking a major step for the company.
    这家初创公司距离推出首款芯片只有几个月了，这标志着公司迈出重要一步。
  2. A adds to growing concerns around B, such as C A 加剧了围绕 B 日益上升的担忧，比如 C
    原文：Anthropic's latest research adds to growing safety concerns around AI, such as the ability for models to carry out cyberattacks…
    The outage adds to growing concerns around cloud reliability, such as single points of failure.
    这次宕机加剧了人们对云服务可靠性的担忧，比如单点故障。
  3. A wouldn't necessarily consist of just B A 未必只由 B 构成
    原文：In practice, an AI worm wouldn't necessarily consist of just one model or strategy.
    In practice, a good study plan wouldn't necessarily consist of just one textbook.
    实际上，好的学习计划未必只靠一本教材。
  4. It's still uncertain exactly what form A will take A 究竟会以什么形式出现，目前还不确定
    原文：It's still "uncertain exactly what form autonomous AI replication will take," the AI lab said…
    It's still uncertain exactly what form the new regulation will take.
    新监管究竟会以什么形式出台，目前还不确定。
