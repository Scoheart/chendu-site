Anthropic pre-IPO meeting hears of surging revenue, no human extinction

Anthropic 路演：Q3 营收飙至 168 亿，调整后已盈利，IPO 估值瞄 2 万亿

By Milana Vinn · Reuters · 2026-10-15 09:49 IST（北京时间 10 月 15 日 16:49；RTE 页更新时间）

今天读路透社（RTE 转载）对 Anthropic 旧金山投资者日的报道。Q3 营收环比增 45% 至 168 亿美元，并称调整后已盈利；Amodei 谈安全却避谈「人类灭绝」，帽子上写着 thinking。重点看：assuage / on an adjusted basis / breakneck / outrun / twelvefold 这类财经与科技套话。

Anthropic has told prospective investors that revenue in the third quarter surged 45%1 from the prior quarter, two people who attended an investor day said, a move that could assuage market concerns2 about the huge costs of developing3 AI systems.

1词汇
surge
激增、飙升。surged 45% = 猛增 45%。比 rose / increased 更猛。名词 a surge in revenue。
3语法
the cost(s) of doing
做某事的成本。the huge costs of developing AI systems = 开发 AI 系统的巨额成本。
2词汇
assuage
缓和、减轻（担忧/痛苦）。assuage concerns = 安抚市场担忧。偏书面。

Third-quarter revenue rose to $16.8 billion4 from $11.6 billion in the second quarter and $4.7 billion in the first quarter, the people said.

4词汇
billion
十亿（美式）。$16.8 billion = 168 亿美元。读作 sixteen point eight billion。

Anthropic told the group the company was profitable on an adjusted basis5 in the third quarter, they added.

5地道表达
on an adjusted basis
按调整后口径。剔除一次性开支等后算「盈利」。财报常用 adjusted / non-GAAP。

The meeting in San Francisco was meant to familiarise prospective6 Anthropic investors with the company before its highly anticipated IPO7.

7词汇
IPO
首次公开募股（Initial Public Offering）。highly anticipated = 备受期待的。
6词汇
familiarise (with)
使熟悉。英式拼写 familiarise（美式 familiarize）。familiarise investors with the company = 让投资者了解公司。

The IPO is expected to precede a public listing8 by rival OpenAI, making Anthropic an early test of investor appetite for9 loss-making AI developers investing heavily to pursue massive scale10. Its public-market debut11 is likely to be pushed until after the November US midterm elections, Reuters has reported.

9地道表达
appetite for
对……的胃口/兴趣。investor appetite for … = 投资者对……的购买意愿。
11地道表达
debut
首次亮相。public-market debut = 公开市场首秀（即上市）。
8词汇
precede
先于、在……之前发生。precede a listing by OpenAI = 抢在 OpenAI 上市之前。
10词汇
pursue
追求、追逐。pursue massive scale = 追求超大规模。AI 公司常说 scale。

Attendees at yesterday's event met with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Chief Financial Officer Krishna Rao and came away optimistic12 about the company's rapid revenue growth and path to profitability13, a few of them told Reuters as they left its headquarters in the evening.

13地道表达
path to profitability
通向盈利之路。创业/AI 融资常用：path to profitability / path to positive cash flow。
12地道表达
come away + adj.
离开时带着某种感受。came away optimistic = 散场时感到乐观。

Amodei reiterated the company’s focus14 on AI safety but avoided mentioning threats of human extinction15, one person in the group said.

15词汇
extinction
灭绝。human extinction = 人类灭绝。AI 安全讨论里的极端风险说法。
14语法
弯引号 ’
注意 company’s 用的是弯引号（’），不是直引号 (')。锚定原文时要一字不差。

In its prospectus seen by Reuters last month, Anthropic warned that advanced AI could pose "catastrophic or existential risks16 to humanity."

16词汇
existential risk
存在性风险（可能威胁人类存续）。prospectus 招股书里的警示语。

Anthropic executives yesterday, however, talked about how the company was using its AI to help its own business including designing a custom chip17. The company handed out hats to the attendees, some of which read "thinking."

17词汇
custom chip
定制芯片。用自家 AI 帮自己设计芯片，显示「AI 赋能业务」叙事。

Anthropic could see its annual revenue run rate18 reach $110 billion by the end of the year, according to estimates from analysts who met with company executives earlier this week.

18地道表达
run rate
年化运行速率。annual revenue run rate = 按当前季度年化后的营收规模（未必等于全年实际）。
[ … ]

Scrutiny of19 AI companies' finances has intensified as they pour money into chips, data centres20 and computing power needed to develop and operate increasingly powerful models.

19词汇
scrutiny
严格审视、审查。Scrutiny of … has intensified = 对……的审视加剧。
20词汇
data centre
数据中心。英式 centres（美式 centers）。chips, data centres and computing power = 芯片、数据中心与算力。

Targeting a valuation of21 over $2 trillion, Anthropic is seeking to convince investors that its breakneck revenue growth22 can eventually outrun the enormous costs23 of building and running AI systems.

21语法
现在分词作状语
句首 Targeting… = 以……估值为目标。新闻里常用分词短语领起背景。
23地道表达
outrun
跑得比……快、超过。outrun the costs = 增长跑赢成本。
22词汇
breakneck
快得危险的、极快的。breakneck growth / speed = 飞奔式增长。

Last year, Anthropic's revenue increased twelvefold24 to nearly $4.6 billion, even as the company lost more than $8 billion on an operating basis25, excluding writedowns of various liabilities26 mostly tied to previous fundraising, Reuters reported exclusively last month.

25地道表达
on an … basis
按……口径。on an operating basis = 按经营口径（未计某些非经常项前）。对照上文 adjusted basis。
24词汇
twelvefold
增至十二倍（增长十一倍）。-fold 表倍数：twofold / tenfold / twelvefold。
26词汇
writedown
减值/减记。writedown of liabilities = 对负债的减记。会计术语。

The company has committed at least $518 billion27 over a decade28 for cloud services, computing and infrastructure.

27词汇
commit
承诺投入。has committed $518 billion = 已承诺至少 5180 亿美元（云与算力合同）。
28地道表达
over a decade
在十年期间。over a decade = 跨越约十年。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A told B that C surged X% from D, a move that could assuage E A 告诉 B：C 较 D 激增 X%，此举可能缓和 E
    原文：Anthropic has told prospective investors that revenue in the third quarter surged 45% from the prior quarter… a move that could assuage market concerns…
    The CFO told analysts that bookings surged 30% from last quarter, a move that could assuage growth concerns.
    CFO 告诉分析师：订单较上季激增 30%，此举可能缓和增长担忧。
  2. A is expected to precede B, making C an early test of D A 预计先于 B，使 C 成为对 D 的早期考验
    原文：The IPO is expected to precede a public listing by rival OpenAI, making Anthropic an early test of investor appetite…
    The chip launch is expected to precede rival releases, making it an early test of demand.
    这款芯片发布预计先于对手，成为需求的早期考验。
  3. A is seeking to convince B that C can eventually outrun D A 试图说服 B：C 最终能跑赢 D
    原文：Anthropic is seeking to convince investors that its breakneck revenue growth can eventually outrun the enormous costs…
    Startups are seeking to convince VCs that efficiency gains can eventually outrun cloud bills.
    创业公司试图说服风投：效率提升最终能跑赢云账单。
  4. A increased N-fold to X, even as B lost Y on a Z basis A 增至 N 倍达到 X，即便 B 按 Z 口径亏损 Y
    原文：Anthropic's revenue increased twelvefold to nearly $4.6 billion, even as the company lost more than $8 billion on an operating basis…
    Sales increased fivefold to $2 billion, even as the firm lost $500 million on an operating basis.
    销售额增至五倍达到 20 亿美元，即便公司按经营口径亏损 5 亿美元。
