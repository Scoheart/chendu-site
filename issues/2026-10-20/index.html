AI agents are only as good as the personal information you share. That raises privacy concerns

AI 智能体越懂你越好用，也越危险：隐私新隐忧

By Wyatte Grantham-Philips · The Associated Press · 2026-10-19 03:17 EDT（北京时间 10 月 19 日 15:17；Yahoo 转载页时间）

今天读美联社商业记者 Wyatte Grantham-Philips 的报道。多伦多科技博主让 Meta 的 AI 智能体 Muse 帮他在 Facebook Marketplace 卖键盘，结果它自作主张成交，还把他家住址告诉了陌生买家。文章借此讨论：智能体掌握的个人信息越多越好用，隐私风险也越大；数据一旦被读取就收不回，连不用智能体的亲友也会被波及。重点看：in the driver’s seat / strike a deal / only as good as / come down to / withdraw access / ripple effects 这类表达。

Matt Robb wanted to sell his computer keyboard. So he made a listing1 on Facebook Marketplace — and, as an experiment2 late last month, he put Meta’s new artificial intelligence agent Muse in the driver’s seat3.

1地道表达
make a listing
发布（商品）信息、挂出售卖。listing /ˈlɪstɪŋ/ n. 列出的商品/房源条目。动词 list：list a keyboard for sale = 把键盘挂出来卖。
3地道表达
in the driver’s seat
坐在驾驶座上 → 掌控局面、说了算。put sb in the driver’s seat = 让某人（这里是 AI）全权主导。同义 in charge / in control。
2地道表达
as an experiment
作为一次试验、试试看。as 表身份/用途。同类：as a test / as a joke。例：I tried the app as an experiment.

Muse got to work4. But within a day, Robb got an alarming update: A buyer he didn’t know had shown up5 outside his house late at night. How? Muse had struck a deal6 on its own and shared Robb’s home address after agreeing to7 a pickup8. It even sent an automated9 “Yep, I’m here!” message from his account when the buyer arrived — all without Robb’s knowledge10.

5语法
过去完成时 + show up
买家「已经出现」在他家门外，发生在 Robb got an alarming update 之前，所以用 had shown up。show up = 露面、出现（口语），同义 turn up。
7语法
after + 动名词（省略主语）
after agreeing to a pickup = after it had agreed to a pickup。动名词 agreeing 的逻辑主语就是句子主语 Muse。agree to + 名词 = 同意（某安排）。
9词汇
automated
/ˈɔːtəmeɪtɪd/ adj. 自动的、自动化的。an automated message = 自动发送的消息。相关：automate v. / automation n.。
4地道表达
get to work
开始干活、着手工作。Muse got to work = Muse 立刻开工。注意不是「到达工作地点」，后者是 get to work 的字面义，靠语境区分。
6地道表达
strike a deal
达成交易、谈妥。strike 过去式 struck。可替换：make / cut / reach a deal。例：The two companies struck a deal worth $2 billion.
8词汇
pickup
/ˈpɪkʌp/ n. 取货、上门自提；接人。来自 pick up。例：The seller offered free local pickup.注意动词短语分写 pick up，名词连写 pickup。
10地道表达
without sb’s knowledge
在某人不知情的情况下。破折号后的 all 指前面「成交、给地址、发消息」这一切。反义 with sb’s knowledge / with sb’s consent（经某人同意）。

Many have welcomed AI agents into their online lives, and raved about11 how personalized assistants like Muse can handle everyday tasks with ease12 — from snagging13 a dinner reservation or planning a trip, to organizing email inboxes14. But like other AI bots, the agents are only as good as the information they have access to15. They perform better the more they know about a user16 — and perhaps also that person’s friends, family and even colleagues. That raises privacy concerns17.

11地道表达
rave about
对……赞不绝口、狂赞。rave /reɪv/ 本义「胡言乱语」，引申为「热烈夸赞」。名词 rave review = 好评如潮。
13词汇
snag
/snæɡ/ v. （口语）抢到、弄到（难得的东西）。snag a dinner reservation = 订到（难订的）晚餐位。名词 snag 另有「小障碍、意外问题」之意：hit a snag。
15语法
be only as good as …
好坏完全取决于……（……多好，它就多好）。句末 to 不能省：have access to the information → the information (that) they have access to，介词留在从句末尾。
17地道表达
raise concerns
引发担忧。raise concerns about sth = 让人担心某事。可替换：spark / prompt / fuel concerns。privacy /ˈpraɪvəsi/ n. 隐私。
12地道表达
with ease
轻松地、毫不费力地 = easily。with + 抽象名词 = 副词：with care（小心地）、with confidence（自信地）。
14语法
from A or B, to C 列举范围
from snagging … or planning …, to organizing … = 从……到……，列举智能体能做的日常事务；from 和 to 都是介词，后接动名词。
16语法
比较级 + the more 后置
They perform better the more they know = The more they know, the better they perform「知道得越多，表现越好」。把 the more 从句放在句末，语气更自然。

Robb and the buyer eventually made amends18, and he learned from Meta that the sharing of his address came down to19 permissions20 he granted when first giving Muse access to21 his Marketplace profile. But the episode showed real-world risks that can arise from22 sharing extensive23 personal information with the technology.

19地道表达
come down to
归根结底在于、取决于。the sharing of his address came down to permissions … = 地址被泄露，说到底是因为他授予的权限。例：It all comes down to trust.
21语法
when + 分词 / give sb access to
when first giving … = when he first gave …，主语相同时可省略主语、用 -ing。give sb access to sth = 让某人能访问某物。
23词汇
extensive
/ɪkˈstensɪv/ adj. 大量的、广泛的。extensive personal information = 大量个人信息。例：The storm caused extensive damage.
18地道表达
make amends
弥补过失、赔不是、和好。amends /əˈmendz/ 总用复数。make amends for sth = 为某事作补偿。例：He tried to make amends for missing her birthday.
20词汇
permission
/pərˈmɪʃən/ n. 许可；（软件）权限，常用复数。grant / revoke permissions = 授予 / 撤销权限。手机弹窗「允许访问通讯录」就是 permission request。
22地道表达
arise from
由……产生、源于。arise /əˈraɪz/（arose, arisen）。risks that can arise from sharing … = 分享信息可能带来的风险。同义 result from / stem from。

“Using an AI agent is similar to handing24 someone your keys and a to-do list25,” said Veronica Rivera, assistant professor at Georgia Tech’s School of Cybersecurity and Privacy. “How helpful they are26, and by extension27 how risky, depends on which keys you hand over28.”

25词汇
to-do list
/təˈduː lɪst/ n. 待办事项清单。连字符把 to-do 变成形容词修饰 list。例：Add it to your to-do list.
27地道表达
by extension
引申开来、由此推及。how helpful they are, and by extension how risky = 有多好用，进而也就有多危险。例：Bad sleep hurts your mood and, by extension, your work.
24语法
be similar to + doing
to 是介词，后接动名词 handing。主语 Using an AI agent 也是动名词：「用 AI 智能体就像把钥匙和待办清单交给别人」。
26语法
名词性从句作主语
How helpful they are, and … how risky, depends on …：how 从句作主语（陈述语序 they are），谓语用单数 depends。how risky 后省略了 they are。
28地道表达
hand over
交出、移交。hand over the keys = 交出钥匙。可拆开：hand the keys over / hand them over（代词必须放中间）。

Once AI agents obtain29 users’ information, it’s very difficult to fully withdraw access30, said Ebrahim Bagheri31, a professor at the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Information.

29词汇
obtain
/əbˈteɪn/ v. 获得、取得（较正式）= get。obtain information / permission / a visa。名词少用，形容词 obtainable。
31语法
引述动词倒装
间接引语在前时，said 常放到主语前：…, said Ebrahim Bagheri。主语带长同位语（a professor at …）时尤其常见；主语是代词时不倒装：…, he said。
30地道表达
withdraw access
撤回访问权限。withdraw /wɪðˈdrɔː/（withdrew, withdrawn）撤回、收回；也指取款：withdraw cash。同义 revoke access。

“If you provide access to your credit card to an agent, the agent now immediately has access to all of the historical transactions32 … they will immediately — within less than seconds — ingest33 all of that data,” Bagheri said. And while users can later withdraw access34, Bagheri said there’s “no taking back35” data the agent has already seen36, and how developers store historical data37 can be murky38.

33词汇
ingest
/ɪnˈdʒest/ v. 摄入（食物）；（数据领域）导入、吸收大量数据。ingest all of that data = 把这些数据全部吞进去。名词 ingestion：data ingestion pipeline。
35地道表达
there’s no taking back …
无法收回……。there is no + doing = 不可能做……：There’s no denying it（无可否认）。take back = 收回（话、东西）。
37语法
how 从句作主语
how developers store historical data can be murky：how 从句（陈述语序）作主语，谓语 can be。「开发者怎么存储历史数据可能说不清」。
32词汇
transaction
/trænˈzækʃən/ n. 交易、（一笔）交易记录。historical transactions = 历史交易记录。例：Check your recent transactions in the app.
34语法
while 表让步
while = although「虽然」。虽然用户事后可以撤回权限，但……。while 也可表「同时」或对比，看语境。
36语法
省略关系代词
= data that the agent has already seen。关系代词在从句中作宾语时可省略。
38词汇
murky
/ˈmɜːrki/ adj. （水）浑浊的；（事情）模糊不清、不透明的。murky rules = 规则含糊。例：The company’s data practices remain murky.
[ … ]

Even those who do not join39 the AI agent boom aren’t necessarily shielded40. Consumers who don’t download an AI agent might still interact with others who do41. And beyond potential confusion when AI sends messages on behalf of42 a user, experts warn of ripple effects43 when it comes to44 privacy.

39语法
those who …
those who = the people who「那些……的人」。Even those who do not join the AI agent boom = 就连不赶这波智能体热潮的人。
41语法
助动词 do 替代
others who do = others who do download an AI agent。用 do 替代前面的动词短语，避免重复。
43地道表达
ripple effect
涟漪效应、连锁反应。ripple /ˈrɪpəl/ n. 涟漪。例：The layoffs had ripple effects across the town.
40词汇
shield
/ʃiːld/ v. 保护、使免受。be shielded from sth = 免受……。aren’t necessarily shielded = 未必能幸免（部分否定）。名词 shield = 盾牌。
42地道表达
on behalf of
代表、替……。AI sends messages on behalf of a user = AI 替用户发消息。常见：on behalf of the team（代表全队）。
44地道表达
when it comes to …
说到……、在……方面。to 是介词，后接名词或动名词：when it comes to cooking。

“When your agent reads your inbox or your texts, it’s also reading what your friends and family sent you45, even if they never46 explicitly47 agreed to that,” Rivera said.

45语法
what 引导宾语从句
what = the things that。reading what your friends and family sent you = 读你亲友发给你的内容。
47词汇
explicitly
/ɪkˈsplɪsɪtli/ adv. 明确地、清楚地。explicitly agree = 明确同意。反义 implicitly（含蓄地、默认地）。
46语法
even if 让步
即使、哪怕。even if they never … agreed to that = 哪怕他们从没同意过。even if 偏假设，even though 偏事实。

adj. 浑浊的；模糊不清、不透明的How developers store historical data can be murky.
extensive
/ɪkˈstensɪv/
adj. 大量的、广泛的The episode showed real-world risks that can arise from sharing extensive personal information with the technology.
explicitly
/ɪkˈsplɪsɪtli/
adv. 明确地、清楚地It’s also reading what your friends and family sent you, even if they never explicitly agreed to that.

可以模仿的句型

  1. A is only as good as B A 的好坏完全取决于 B
    原文：But like other AI bots, the agents are only as good as the information they have access to.
    A translation app is only as good as the data it was trained on.
    翻译软件好不好用，完全取决于训练它的数据。
  2. A does X better the more B … B 越……，A 就做得越好（the more 从句后置）
    原文：They perform better the more they know about a user — and perhaps also that person’s friends, family and even colleagues.
    Music apps get better at recommending songs the more you listen.
    你听得越多，音乐 App 推荐歌曲就越准。
  3. Doing A is similar to doing B. How …, and by extension how …, depends on … 做 A 就像做 B；有多……、进而有多……，取决于……
    原文：“Using an AI agent is similar to handing someone your keys and a to-do list,” said Veronica Rivera, assistant professor at Georgia Tech’s School of Cybersecurity and Privacy. “How helpful they are, and by extension how risky, depends on which keys you hand over.”
    Learning a language is similar to building a house. How fast you progress, and by extension how far you go, depends on the foundation you lay.
    学语言就像盖房子：进步多快、进而能走多远，取决于你打下的地基。
