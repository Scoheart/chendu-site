Most US stocks rise following strong profit reports from Equifax, Hasbro and other big companies

财报给力、油价回落：道指大涨 1%，AI 股仍在震荡

By Stan Choe · The Associated Press · 2026-10-20 16:50 EDT（北京时间 10 月 21 日 04:50；WTOP 转载页时间）

今天读美联社商业记者 Stan Choe 的收盘报道（美东 10 月 20 日周二）。Equifax、孩之宝、3M 等财报好于预期，布伦特原油从逼近 112 美元回落到 108.79 美元，标普 500 涨 0.3%、道指涨 1%；通用汽车利润超预期却因全年指引不及预期大跌 6.8%；AI 股因高利率可能拖累借贷买芯片而继续震荡，纳指微跌。重点看：ease back / rally / hit records / get a lift / yo-yo / keep in check / fall short of / give way to 这类财经表达。

NEW YORK (AP) — Most of the U.S. stock market rose Tuesday following strong profit reports1 from Equifax, Hasbro and other companies, while oil prices eased back2 after nearing their highest levels since3 the war with Iran began.

1语法
following 作介词
following 在这里是介词，= after（在……之后、由于……）：「在强劲的利润报告公布之后」。新闻里常用 following the announcement / following the news。profit report = 利润报告，即财报。
3语法
after + doing；near 作动词
after nearing … = after they neared …，介词 after 后接动名词，省略了主语（与主句的 oil prices 一致）。near 在这里是动词「接近」：near a record = 逼近纪录。since the war with Iran began = 自对伊朗战争开始以来。
2地道表达
ease back
（价格）回落、小幅回调。oil prices eased back = 油价回落。ease = 缓和、放松；back 表示回到原来的水平。同义 pull back / retreat。例：Bond yields eased back after the data.

The S&P 500 added 0.3%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average rallied4 529 points, or 1%, while more swings for stocks in the artificial-intelligence industry sent the Nasdaq composite to a modest loss5 of less than 0.1%.

5地道表达
send sth to …
使……落到（某种状态）。sent the Nasdaq to a modest loss = 让纳指小幅收跌。modest /ˈmɑːdɪst/ adj. 不大的、温和的：a modest gain / loss = 小涨 / 小跌。同类：send stocks higher / lower。
4词汇
rally
/ˈræli/ v./n. （股市）反弹、大涨。The Dow rallied 529 points = 道指大涨 529 点。名词：a relief rally（解脱性反弹）。例：Stocks rallied after the Fed held rates steady.

Stock prices tend to follow the path of6 corporate profits over the long term7, and several companies delivered results for the latest quarter that were better than analysts expected8. Big expectations for growth generally are what have allowed9 the U.S. stock market to hit records10 recently despite worries about high inflation, higher interest rates and other challenges weighing on Wall Street11.

7地道表达
over the long term
从长期来看。反义 in the short term（短期内）。投资常说 over the long run 同义。例：Over the long term, stocks have beaten bonds.
9语法
what 引导表语从句
主语 Big expectations for growth，系动词 are，what have allowed … 是表语从句：「对增长的高预期，正是让美股……的原因」。what = the thing(s) that。allow sb/sth to do = 使……得以。
11语法
现在分词后置定语
weighing on Wall Street 修饰 challenges = challenges that are weighing on Wall Street：「压在华尔街身上的挑战」。weigh on = 拖累、压制。
6地道表达
follow the path of
沿着……的路径走，即「跟随……的走势」。Stock prices tend to follow the path of corporate profits = 股价往往跟随企业利润走。tend to do = 往往、倾向于。
8语法
比较状语从句
better than analysts expected = 比分析师预期的好。than 后面是从句（analysts expected），expected 后省略了宾语。财报常说 beat expectations / better than expected / missed estimates。
10地道表达
hit records / a record
创纪录。the stock market hit records = 股市屡创新高。同义 set a record / hit an all-time high。

Equifax rallied 11.3% for one of the market’s biggest gains12 after reporting a bigger profit for the summer than analysts expected, despite high mortgage rates13 slowing some parts of its business14.

13词汇
mortgage
/ˈmɔːrɡɪdʒ/ n. 抵押贷款、房贷。注意 t 不发音。mortgage rates = 房贷利率。Equifax 是征信公司，房贷申请少了，征信查询业务也会放缓。
12语法
for 表「算作、成为」
Equifax rallied 11.3% for one of the market’s biggest gains = 大涨 11.3%，成为市场涨幅最大的股票之一。for 引出结果/归类。one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。
14语法
despite + 名词 + 现在分词
despite 是介词，后接复合结构 high mortgage rates slowing …（高房贷利率拖慢……），名词是逻辑主语，现在分词是逻辑谓语。= although high mortgage rates slowed …。

Hasbro climbed 11.2% after the toy company said its “Magic: The Gathering” game made more than $600 million in revenue15 during a quarter for the first time. 3M rose 5.8% after the company behind Ace bandages and Scotch-Brite sponges16 likewise17 reported a stronger-than-expected18 profit, even as shoppers broadly feel discouraged19 by high inflation.

15词汇
revenue
/ˈrevənuː/ n. 收入、营收。made more than $600 million in revenue = 营收超过 6 亿美元。区分 revenue（营收，未扣成本）和 profit（利润）。
17词汇
likewise
/ˈlaɪkwaɪz/ adv. 同样地、也。3M likewise reported a stronger-than-expected profit = 3M 同样交出了好于预期的利润。同义 similarly / also。
19语法
even as 让步
even as = even though / while，「尽管、即便在……的同时」。强调两件事同时发生且形成反差：利润好于预期，而消费者普遍情绪低落。discouraged /dɪsˈkɜːrɪdʒd/ adj. 灰心的。
16地道表达
the company behind …
……背后的公司，即「……的生产商/品牌方」。新闻用产品让读者认出公司：3M 就是做 Ace 弹力绷带和思高（Scotch-Brite）百洁布的公司。例：the company behind ChatGPT。
18语法
连字符复合形容词
stronger-than-expected profit = 好于预期的利润。用连字符把一个短语变成前置形容词。财经常见：better-than-expected / weaker-than-expected / long-awaited。

Stocks got a lift20 after the price for a barrel of Brent crude21 erased an early gain22 to fall 0.7% to $108.79. Earlier in the morning, it got near $112 and approached its highest price since the war with Iran began in late February.

21词汇
barrel / Brent crude
barrel /ˈbærəl/ n. 桶（石油计量单位，约 159 升）。Brent crude = 布伦特原油，国际油价基准；美国基准是 WTI。crude /kruːd/ = 原油（crude oil）。
20地道表达
get a lift
得到提振。Stocks got a lift = 股市获得提振。lift n. 提升。也说 give stocks a lift（提振股市）、a boost。
22地道表达
erase a gain
抹去（早盘）涨幅。erased an early gain to fall 0.7% = 回吐早盘涨幅，转跌 0.7%。to fall 是表结果的不定式。反义 erase losses（收复跌幅）。

The drop in oil prices will relieve some of the pressure on inflation23, if it lasts24. Brent’s price has been yo-yoing25 between $97 and $112 per barrel this month on uncertainty about26 when the energy industry will return to normal27.

23词汇
relieve
/rɪˈliːv/ v. 缓解、减轻。relieve pressure = 减压。名词 relief：a relief rally。油价下跌会减轻通胀压力。
25词汇
yo-yo
/ˈjoʊjoʊ/ v. 像溜溜球一样上下波动。Brent’s price has been yo-yoing between $97 and $112 = 布油价格在 97 到 112 美元之间来回震荡。现在完成进行时表示「这个月一直在……」。同义 swing / seesaw。
27地道表达
return to normal
恢复正常。when the energy industry will return to normal = 能源行业何时恢复常态。是 about 后面的宾语从句，用陈述语序。
24语法
条件状语后置
if it lasts = 如果（油价下跌）能持续的话。放在句尾表示补充条件、带保留语气。last v. 持续。类似 if it holds / if that continues。
26语法
on 表原因
on uncertainty about … = 由于对……的不确定性。财经新闻常用 on 表原因：Stocks fell on recession fears.Oil rose on supply worries。
[ … ]

Keeping the market in check was General Motors28, which sank29 6.8% despite reporting a bigger profit30 than analysts expected. It also announced a program to send up to $20 billion to investors by buying back its stock31.

29词汇
sink
/sɪŋk/ v. 下沉；（股价）大跌。sink-sank-sunk。GM sank 6.8% = 通用汽车大跌 6.8%。同类：tumble / slide / drop。
31词汇
buy back / buyback
回购（股票）。send up to $20 billion to investors by buying back its stock = 通过回购股票向投资者返还最多 200 亿美元。名词 buyback：a $20 billion buyback。
28语法
倒装：分词短语 + was + 主语
正常语序：General Motors was keeping the market in check。把分词短语提前、主语后置，强调「拖住大盘的是通用汽车」。keep sth in check = 控制、抑制（这里指限制涨幅）。
30语法
despite + doing
despite 是介词，后接动名词 reporting（不能接从句；接从句要用 although）。「尽管公布的利润高于预期」。

The pressure is on companies to deliver32 bigger profits not only now, but also in the future33. GM gave a forecasted range for profit over the full year of 2026 whose midpoint34 fell short of35 analysts’ expectations.

33语法
not only … but also …
不仅……而且……，连接两个时间状语 now 和 in the future。强调市场不仅看当期业绩，也看未来指引（guidance）。
35地道表达
fall short of
未达到、不及（预期、目标）。fell short of analysts’ expectations = 不及分析师预期。同义 miss。反义 beat / exceed expectations。
32地道表达
the pressure is on sb to do
某人面临要……的压力。The pressure is on companies to deliver bigger profits = 企业面临交出更多利润的压力。deliver = 兑现、交出（成绩）。
34语法
whose 定语从句
whose midpoint fell short of … 修饰 a forecasted range：「（全年利润预测区间）其中值低于分析师预期」。whose 可指物，= of which。midpoint n. 中值。

AI stocks, meanwhile, continued to swirl36. They’ve been at the center of37 the market’s recent uneasiness, as euphoria38 around the technology’s boom gives way to39 worries that high interest rates40 in the bond market will slow the borrowing that’s allowed companies to buy41 so many AI chips. Last week was the U.S. stock market’s worst since June42 because of weakness for AI-related companies.

37地道表达
at the center of
处于……的中心、是……的焦点。at the center of the market’s recent uneasiness = 是近期市场不安的核心。uneasiness /ʌnˈiːzinəs/ n. 不安、忧虑。
39地道表达
give way to
让位于、被……取代。euphoria … gives way to worries = 狂热让位于担忧。例：Optimism gave way to fear as prices fell.
41语法
that’s = that has（定语从句）
that’s allowed … 修饰 the borrowing，that’s 是 that has 的缩写（后接过去分词 allowed，构成现在完成时）。「让企业得以买下这么多 AI 芯片的借贷」。
36词汇
swirl
/swɜːrl/ v. 打旋、翻腾。AI stocks continued to swirl = AI 股继续剧烈震荡、方向不明。比喻股价像漩涡一样来回打转。
38词汇
euphoria
/juːˈfɔːriə/ n. 狂热、极度兴奋。euphoria around the technology’s boom = 围绕 AI 繁荣的狂热情绪。形容词 euphoric。股市常说 market euphoria（市场亢奋）。
40语法
同位语从句
that high interest rates … will slow the borrowing … 解释 worries 的内容：「担心债市高利率会拖慢借贷」。同位语从句的 that 不作成分，不能省略。
42语法
最高级后省略名词
the stock market’s worst (week) since June = 美股自 6 月以来最糟糕的一周。前文有 Last week，所以省略 week。since 表「自……以来」。

    that’s allowed … 修饰 the borrowing，that’s 是 that has 的缩写（后接过去分词 allowed，构成现在完成时）。「让企业得以买下这么多 AI 芯片的借贷」。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A is/are what has/have allowed B to do C despite D 正是 A 让 B 得以做 C，尽管有 D（what 表语从句强调原因）
    原文：Big expectations for growth generally are what have allowed the U.S. stock market to hit records recently despite worries about high inflation, higher interest rates and other challenges weighing on Wall Street.
    Strong demand for AI chips is what has allowed the company to keep raising prices despite worries about a slowing economy.
    正是对 AI 芯片的强劲需求，让这家公司在经济放缓的担忧下仍能不断提价。
  2. Keeping A in check was B, which … 拖住 A 的是 B，它……（倒装强调）
    原文：Keeping the market in check was General Motors, which sank 6.8% despite reporting a bigger profit than analysts expected.
    Keeping the rally in check was Apple, which slipped 2% despite reporting record iPhone sales.
    拖住涨势的是苹果，尽管 iPhone 销量创纪录，其股价仍下跌 2%。
  3. The pressure is on A to do B not only now, but also in the future A 面临的压力是：不仅现在要做到 B，将来也要做到
    原文：The pressure is on companies to deliver bigger profits not only now, but also in the future.
    The pressure is on the new CEO to cut costs not only this year, but also over the next decade.
    新 CEO 面临的压力是：不仅今年要削减成本，未来十年也要做到。
