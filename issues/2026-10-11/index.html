Wall Street finishes its record-setting week with gains

美股收官周收涨：油价悠悠、国债收益率摇摆、电信股遭 SpaceX 冲击

By Stan Choe · The Associated Press · 2026-10-09 13:16 PDT（北京时间 10 月 10 日 04:16；10 月 10 日各报转载）

今天读美联社（AP）周五收盘综述。记录周收涨，但油价 yo-yo、国债收益率来回翻、无线运营商大跌——典型“指数涨、板块散”的一天。重点看：yo-yo / flipped up and down / eked out / the pressure is on 这类财经套话。

NEW YORK (AP) -- The U.S. stock market finished its record-setting week1 with gains on Friday, even as other financial markets remained indecisive2.

1词汇
record-setting
创纪录的。record-setting week = 创下多项纪录的一周（指数曾刷历史新高）。也说 a record week。
2词汇
indecisive
犹豫不决的、方向不明的。这里写油市/债市没有明确涨跌方向，和股市“收涨”形成对比。

The S&P 500 climbed 0.6% for its first gain since setting its all-time high3 three days earlier. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 423 points, or 0.8%, and the Nasdaq composite rose 0.6%.

3词汇
all-time high
历史最高点。set / hit / top an all-time high。反义 all-time low。和上一期美股晨读重复出现，刻意巩固。

The strength came as oil prices continued to yo-yo4 because of uncertainty about when the war with Iran will allow the global energy industry to return to normal. The price for a barrel of Brent crude bounced between $102.50 and $1055 before settling at $104.726, up 0.4%.

5语法
bounce between A and B
在 A 与 B 之间来回跳。财经新闻写区间震荡的常用动词：bounce / pinball / oscillate between。
4地道表达
yo-yo (v.)
像悠悠球一样上下晃 → 来回剧烈波动。比 pinballing 更口语。名词：a yo-yo market。
6地道表达
settle at
收于、定格在（某价位）。oil / futures settled at… = 结算价/收盘价。settle 在商品市场几乎是固定搭配。

Treasury yields flipped up and down7 in the bond market following their own sharp moves earlier in the week. The yield on the 10-year Treasury eventually climbed to 5.24% from 5.22% late Thursday.

7地道表达
flip up and down
来回翻转、上下翻腾。写收益率/价格日内反复变向。比 went up and down 更有画面感。

High yields threaten to slow the economy8 by making borrowing money more expensive for everyone, and the average 30-year fixed-rate mortgage has already leaped to its highest level9 in nearly three years.

9词汇
leap
猛跳、跃升。leap to a high = 一下子蹿到高位。现在完成时 has leaped，强调已经发生且影响仍在。
8语法
threaten to do
有…的危险、可能会…。主语常是抽象事物（high yields / inflation）。不是“威胁某人”，而是“预示坏结果”。
[ … ]

On Wall Street, Humana rallied 11.6% and helped lift the U.S. stock market despite the swings10 in the oil and bond markets. The insurer said it received encouraging data about its quality ratings versus its competitors for Medicare Advantage plans.

10语法
despite + noun
尽管有…。despite the swings = 尽管波动很大。后面直接跟名词短语，不加 of 从句（那是 in spite of the fact that…）。

Companies that own cell towers were also at the front of the market11, including leaps of 15.6%12 for Crown Castle and 9.3% for American Tower.

11地道表达
at the front of the market
领涨、站在涨幅前列。= among the day’s biggest gainers。反义 at the back / lagged。
12词汇
leap (n.)
大幅上涨（名词）。leaps of 15.6% = 暴涨 15.6%。涨跌名词族：leap / jump / surge vs drop / slide / plunge。

They rallied after SpaceX announced a purchase that could help it become a major mobile carrier in the United States. It still needs to get approval from U.S. regulators, but SpaceX said the purchase of low-band spectrum13 would allow its Starlink Mobile business to have a signal that can penetrate walls14 and connect customers inside buildings who may not be able to access its satellites. SpaceX’s stock added 1.2%.

13词汇
spectrum
无线电频谱。low-band = 低频段（穿透力强，适合室内覆盖）。电信/卫星新闻高频词。
14词汇
penetrate
穿透。信号 penetrate walls = 穿墙。也可用于市场：penetrate a new market 打入新市场。

The threat of another big competitor sent stocks lower across the wireless industry15, including drops of 13.3% for T-Mobile US, 9.8% for AT&T and 8.7% for Verizon.

15语法
send sth lower / higher
把…推低/推高。send stocks lower = 导致股价下跌。across the industry = 全行业范围内。

The pressure is on companies broadly16 to deliver strong growth in profits after their stock prices ran toward records17 despite the downward push from high bond yields. Next week will bring high-profile profit reports18 from the country’s biggest banks, including Citigroup, JPMorgan Chase and Wells Fargo all on Tuesday.

17地道表达
run toward records
一路奔向纪录高位。run 这里不是“跑步”，而是价格快速上行。despite the downward push = 尽管有向下的推力。
16地道表达
the pressure is on
压力来了、该拿出表现了。broadly = 普遍地、整体上。= Companies are under broad pressure to…
18词汇
high-profile
高调的、备受关注的。high-profile earnings / cases / visits。这里指大银行财报，市场盯得很紧。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A finished … with gains, even as B remained … A 收涨收官，尽管 B 仍…
    原文：The U.S. stock market finished its record-setting week with gains on Friday, even as other financial markets remained indecisive.
    The team finished the season with wins, even as injuries remained a worry.
    球队以连胜收官赛季，尽管伤病仍是隐患。
  2. A continued to yo-yo because of uncertainty about … 因对…的不确定，A 继续上下晃
    原文：…oil prices continued to yo-yo because of uncertainty about when the war with Iran will allow the global energy industry to return to normal.
    Bitcoin continued to yo-yo because of uncertainty about the next rate decision.
    比特币因下次利率决议的不确定而继续剧烈波动。
  3. The threat of A sent B lower across C A 的威胁把 C 范围内的 B 集体打低
    原文：The threat of another big competitor sent stocks lower across the wireless industry…
    The threat of new regulation sent shares lower across the fintech industry.
    新监管的威胁让金融科技板块股价普遍下跌。
  4. The pressure is on sb to do sth after … 在…之后，某人面临必须做出…的压力
    原文：The pressure is on companies broadly to deliver strong growth in profits after their stock prices ran toward records…
    The pressure is on the startup to show real users after its valuation ran toward records.
    估值冲向纪录之后，这家创业公司面临必须拿出真实用户的压力。
