Sharp drops for AI stocks overwhelm markets worldwide as oil prices jump

AI 股又一次「哆嗦」：纳指暴跌 2.6%，美债收益率大跌

By Stan Choe · The Associated Press · 2026-10-22 17:07 EDT（北京时间 10 月 23 日 05:07；WTOP 转载页时间）

今天读美联社商业记者 Stan Choe 的收盘报道（美东 10 月 22 日周四）。泛林集团财报好于预期却暴跌 11.8%，Meta 跌 7%，英伟达跌 4.4% 成为拖累标普最大的单一力量；纳指跌 2.6%、标普跌 1.2%，但标普成分股里其实多数上涨。投资者转向避险，10 年期美债收益率从 5.13% 跌到 4.97%。重点看：shudder / tumble / mask / under threat / shoot too high / carry weight / in search of / even as 这类财经表达。

NEW YORK (AP) — The latest shudder1 for stocks in the artificial-intelligence industry pulled markets lower2 worldwide on Thursday, while oil prices hit their highest levels since May on worries about3 the war with Iran.

1词汇
shudder
/ˈʃʌdər/ n./v. 颤抖、哆嗦。The latest shudder for stocks = 股市的又一次「哆嗦」，比喻突然的剧烈下跌。名词复数 shudders 也常见：send shudders through the market（让市场一阵发抖）。
3语法
on 表原因
on worries about the war with Iran = 由于对伊朗战争的担忧。财经新闻常用 on + 名词 表示原因：Oil rose on supply fears.上一期 on uncertainty about 是同一用法。
2地道表达
pull … lower
把……拉低。pulled markets lower worldwide = 拖累全球股市走低。反义 push/lift … higher。例：Tech stocks pulled the index lower.

The S&P 500 fell 1.2% for its worst day since July4, while the Nasdaq composite tumbled5 2.6%. The weakness for tech stocks was so overwhelming that6 it masked strength for the rest of7 the U.S. market, where the majority of stocks actually rose8. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 11 points, or less than 0.1%.

5词汇
tumble
/ˈtʌmbəl/ v. 暴跌、跌倒。the Nasdaq composite tumbled 2.6% = 纳指暴跌 2.6%。比 fall / drop 更猛烈。同类：plunge / sink / slide。
7地道表达
mask sth
掩盖、遮住（某种真实情况）。masked strength for the rest of the U.S. market = 掩盖了美股其他部分的强势。mask 原义「面具」。例：Strong sales masked weak profits.
4语法
for 表「算作、结果」
The S&P 500 fell 1.2% for its worst day since July = 标普下跌 1.2%，成为 7 月以来最糟糕的一天。for 引出这次下跌带来的结果/归类。
6语法
so … that … 结果状语从句
The weakness … was so overwhelming that it masked … = 科技股的疲软如此压倒性，以至于掩盖了……。overwhelming /ˌoʊvərˈwelmɪŋ/ adj. 压倒性的、势不可挡的。
8语法
where 非限制性定语从句
where 指代 the rest of the U.S. market，= in which：「而在那（其余市场）里，大多数股票其实是上涨的」。actually 表示「出人意料地、事实上」。the majority of = 大多数。

AI-related stocks have been swinging on worries that9 their boom may be under threat10 because of how expensive it’s become to borrow money11 to build data centers and buy more AI chips. Concerns also remain that their prices shot too high12 following a yearslong frenzy13, even though companies in the industry continue to report growth.

9语法
现在完成进行时 + 同位语从句
have been swinging = 一直在大幅摇摆（持续到现在）。worries that their boom may be under threat … 是同位语从句，说明担忧的内容。swing v. 摆动；股价 swing = 大起大落。
11语法
how 宾语从句 + 形式主语 it
because of 后接 how 引导的名词性从句：「因为借钱已经变得多么昂贵」。it’s = it has；it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to borrow money …。句型 it has become + adj. + to do。
13词汇
frenzy
/ˈfrenzi/ n. 狂热、疯狂。a yearslong frenzy = 持续数年的狂热。yearslong 是合成形容词，= lasting years。同义 euphoria / mania。例：a buying frenzy（抢购狂潮）。
10地道表达
under threat
受到威胁。their boom may be under threat = 它们的繁荣可能受到威胁。under + 名词表示处于某种状态：under pressure / under review / under way。
12地道表达
shoot too high
（价格）冲得太高、涨过头。shoot-shot-shot。their prices shot too high = 估值涨过头了。shoot up = 飙升：Oil prices shot up.

Lam Research, whose equipment and services14 help make semiconductors15, reported gains in profit and revenue for the summer that were slightly stronger than analysts expected16, for example. Usually, that helps boost a stock’s price, but Lam Research’s tumbled17 11.8%.

15词汇
semiconductor
/ˌsemikənˈdʌktər/ n. 半导体；芯片。semi- = 半，conductor = 导体。semiconductor stocks = 半导体股。口语常说 chips。
17语法
所有格后省略名词
Lam Research’s tumbled 11.8% = Lam Research’s (stock price) tumbled，前面刚说过 a stock’s price，所以省略 stock price，避免重复。
14语法
whose 非限制性定语从句
Lam Research, whose equipment and services help make semiconductors, … = 泛林集团（其设备和服务帮助制造半导体）……。用插入的 whose 从句给读者介绍公司是做什么的。help (to) do，to 可省。
16语法
比较状语从句
slightly stronger than analysts expected = 比分析师预期的略好。slightly 修饰比较级表「稍微」；也可用 much / far / a bit。expected 后省略宾语。

It gave a range for its forecast for profit this quarter whose midpoint was slightly below18 analysts’ expectations. Its stock also came into the day with19 a 52.4% surge for the year so far, nearly quadruple20 the gain for the overall S&P 500, leaving plenty of room for potential disappointment21.

19地道表达
come into the day with
带着……进入这一天（开盘前已经……）。Its stock came into the day with a 52.4% surge for the year so far = 该股在当天之前今年已累计大涨 52.4%。surge /sɜːrdʒ/ n. 飙升。
21语法
现在分词作结果状语
leaving … 表示前面情况带来的结果：「（涨了这么多）留下了很大的失望空间」，即预期太高，稍有不及就容易下跌。room for = ……的余地。
18语法
whose 定语从句（指物）
a range for its forecast … whose midpoint was slightly below analysts’ expectations = 给出的利润预测区间，其中值略低于分析师预期。whose 可指物，= of which。股市看重的是业绩指引（guidance）。
20词汇
quadruple
/kwɑːˈdruːpl/ adj./v. 四倍（的）。nearly quadruple the gain for the overall S&P 500 = 接近标普 500 整体涨幅的四倍。double 两倍、triple 三倍、quadruple 四倍。

Meta Platforms, which is pushing its Muse AI agent22, lost 7% after reporting stronger revenue for the latest quarter than analysts expected, though its profit fell short23. The company behind Facebook and Instagram also said it’s creating a new class of stock24 to raise money to pay for25 AI investments and other expenses.

23语法
though 让步 + fall short
though its profit fell short (of expectations) = 不过利润未达预期。fall short 后面省略了 of analysts’ expectations。though 放在句末补充转折。
25语法
不定式表目的（连用）
creating a new class of stock to raise money to pay for AI investments = 设立新股以筹钱、再用来支付 AI 投资。两个 to do 层层递进：to raise（目的）→ to pay for（钱的用途）。raise money = 筹资。
22语法
which 非限制性定语从句
插入补充信息：Meta Platforms（它正在力推自家的 Muse AI 智能体）。push 在这里是「大力推广」。agent n. 智能体、代理。
24词汇
class of stock
股票类别。a new class of stock = 新的一类股票（这里指无投票权股票）。class A / class B shares = A 类、B 类股，投票权不同。

The global tide against26 AI stocks knocked Nvidia down 4.4%27. Because the chip giant is the largest company on Wall Street by total value28, movements for its stock carry more weight29 on the S&P 500 than any other30, and it was the single strongest force pulling the index lower31.

27地道表达
knock sth down
把……打下来，即「使……下跌」。knocked Nvidia down 4.4% = 让英伟达跌了 4.4%。主语是「浪潮」这种抽象事物，拟人化写法。
29地道表达
carry weight
有分量、影响大。movements for its stock carry more weight on the S&P 500 = 它的股价波动对标普 500 影响更大（标普按市值加权）。同义 matter more / have more influence。
31语法
single 强调最高级 + 分词后置定语
the single strongest force = 唯一最大的力量，single 加强最高级语气。pulling the index lower 修饰 force = force that was pulling the index lower。
26地道表达
the tide against …
针对……的潮流/浪潮。The global tide against AI stocks = 全球抛售 AI 股的浪潮。the tide turned = 形势逆转。
28词汇
total value（市值）
by total value = 按总价值（即市值 market value / market capitalization）计算。the largest company on Wall Street by total value = 华尔街市值最大的公司。
30语法
比较级 + than any other
carry more weight … than any other (company) = 比其他任何公司都更有分量，用比较级表达最高级意思。注意 any other 后通常接单数名词，这里省略了 company。
[ … ]

But U.S. yields nevertheless32 fell sharply Thursday in a signal that33 investors were buying U.S. government bonds in search of something safer to own34 as AI stocks tumbled.

33语法
in a signal that（同位语从句）
in a signal that investors were buying U.S. government bonds = 这表明投资者在买入美国国债。that 从句解释 signal 的内容，属同位语从句。也可写 a sign that …。
32词汇
nevertheless
/ˌnevərðəˈles/ adv. 然而、尽管如此。U.S. yields nevertheless fell sharply = 尽管如此，美债收益率还是大幅下跌。比 however 正式；同义 nonetheless。
34地道表达
in search of
寻找、为了寻求。in search of something safer to own = 寻找更安全的资产来持有（避险）。something + adj. + to do：形容词后置修饰 something。

The yield on the 10-year Treasury35 tumbled to 4.97% from 5.13%36 late Wednesday and from more than 5.30% earlier this month. That’s a major move37 for the bond market.

35词汇
yield / Treasury
yield /jiːld/ n. 收益率。Treasury /ˈtreʒəri/ n.美国国债（大写，常指 Treasury notes/bonds）。10-year Treasury yield = 10 年期美债收益率。债券价格与收益率反向变动。
37地道表达
a major move
大幅波动。move 在市场语境里 = 价格变动。That’s a major move for the bond market = 对债市来说这是很大的变动。同类：a big move / a sharp move。
36语法
to … from …
tumbled to 4.97% from 5.13% = 从 5.13% 跌到 4.97%。英文财经新闻常把 to（新数值）放在 from（旧数值）前面，突出最新结果。

Thursday’s drop in yields came even as38 another report suggested the U.S. economy may be doing better than economists expected. It said fewer U.S. workers applied for unemployment benefits39 last week, which could signal that40 layoffs41 remain low across the country.

39地道表达
apply for unemployment benefits
申领失业救济金。unemployment benefits = 失业救济。每周的「初请失业金人数」(initial jobless claims) 就是看有多少人 applied for unemployment benefits。
41词汇
layoff
/ˈleɪɔːf/ n. 裁员。layoffs remain low = 裁员人数仍然很低。动词短语 lay off（解雇）：The company laid off 500 workers.
38语法
A came even as B
Thursday’s drop in yields came even as another report suggested … = 周四收益率下跌，而与此同时另一份报告却显示……。even as 强调两件事同时发生且形成反差。
40语法
which 指代前面整件事
which 指代前面「申领失业金的人减少」这件事，引导非限制性定语从句：「这可能表明……」。signal v. 预示、表明。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A was so overwhelming that it masked B A 如此压倒性，以至于掩盖了 B（so … that 结果从句）
    原文：The weakness for tech stocks was so overwhelming that it masked strength for the rest of the U.S. market, where the majority of stocks actually rose.
    The demand for AI chips was so overwhelming that it masked weakness in the rest of the company’s business.
    对 AI 芯片的需求如此强劲，以至于掩盖了公司其他业务的疲软。
  2. Usually, that helps boost A, but B … 通常这会推高 A，但 B 却……（先说常理，再说反常）
    原文：Usually, that helps boost a stock’s price, but Lam Research’s tumbled 11.8%.
    Usually, a rate cut helps boost bank stocks, but this time they slid 3%.
    通常降息会推高银行股，但这一次它们却下跌了 3%。
  3. Because A is …, B carry more weight on C than any other, and it was the single strongest force … 因为 A 是……，B 对 C 的影响比其他任何一个都大，它是……的唯一最大力量
    原文：Because the chip giant is the largest company on Wall Street by total value, movements for its stock carry more weight on the S&P 500 than any other, and it was the single strongest force pulling the index lower.
    Because oil is the country’s biggest export, swings in its price carry more weight on the economy than any other, and it was the single strongest force behind last year’s slowdown.
    因为石油是该国最大的出口商品，油价波动对经济的影响比其他任何因素都大，它是去年经济放缓背后最主要的单一力量。
  4. A came even as B suggested … A 发生的同时，B 却显示……（even as 表反差）
    原文：Thursday’s drop in yields came even as another report suggested the U.S. economy may be doing better than economists expected.
    The sell-off came even as the latest data suggested consumers are still spending freely.
    抛售发生之际，最新数据却显示消费者仍在大方花钱。
