US stocks hover near their latest record in mixed trading on Wall Street

美股忙周开局：指数分化、科技股拖累、油价与 CPI 成焦点

By Damian J. Troise and Alex Veiga · The Associated Press · 2026-10-12 16:47 EDT（北京时间 10 月 13 日 04:47）

今天读美联社（AP）周一收盘综述。标普微跌、道指收涨、纳指走弱——能源涨、芯片跌，典型“指数分化”。重点看：wavering / dragged … into the red / frenzy / closely-watched / settle at 这类财经套话。

Wall Street kicked off a busy week1 on Monday with a mixed finish2 for the major U.S. stock indexes, although the market remained near its all-time high3.

1地道表达
kick off
开场、拉开序幕。kick off a week / meeting / season。本义踢球开球，财经新闻写“一周交易开局”极常用。
3词汇
all-time high
历史最高点。remain near its all-time high = 仍贴近历史新高。和 10/11 期美股晨读重复巩固。
2词汇
mixed
涨跌互现的、结果不一的。a mixed finish / session = 三大指数有涨有跌。反义 broad rally / selloff。

The S&P 500 slipped 0.1% after wavering between small gains and losses4. The benchmark index5 set a record high last Tuesday and is up 14% so far this year.

5词汇
benchmark
基准（指数）。the benchmark index 常特指标普 500。benchmark rate = 基准利率。
4语法
waver between A and B
在 A 与 B 之间摇摆不定。写盘中反复翻红翻绿：waver / oscillate / pinball between gains and losses。

The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 0.4%, and the Nasdaq composite fell 0.4%.

Shares in roughly 64% of the companies in the S&P 500 rose, led by communication services stocks6. Meta Platforms rose 3.4%, T-Mobile added 2.2%, AT&T gained 1.4% and Verizon Communications rose 2.7%.

6语法
led by
由…领涨/领跌。被动结构：Shares rose, led by X = 在 X 带动下上涨。财经开篇固定句式。

Energy stocks had some of the biggest gains as oil prices kept rising. Chevron rose 1.4%.

Despite those gains, a slide in big technology stocks dragged the market into the red7.

7地道表达
into the red
跌入亏损区、翻绿。drag A into the red = 把 A 拖进下跌。反义 into the black（翻红盈利）。

Micron Technology fell 3.9%, Advanced Micro Devices dropped 2.3%, Broadcom slid 1.9% and Nvidia gave up 1.3%8. They are among several companies in the sector with big stock values that tend to have a heavy influence on the market9. They have also been the driving force behind10 the broader market’s record gains throughout the year.

9词汇
heavy influence
重大影响。大市值股 have a heavy influence = 权重高、牵一发动全身。= outsized / outsize influence。
8地道表达
give up (gains)
回吐、跌去。gave up 1.3% = 跌了 1.3%（常暗示此前涨过又吐回去）。也说 gave back / surrendered。
10地道表达
driving force
推动力、主力。the driving force behind the rally = 本轮上涨的核心推手。

Those companies could face more scrutiny11 from investors during this round of corporate earnings as Wall Street tries to get a better sense of whether the stock gains made during the frenzy over artificial intelligence12 can translate into future growth13.

11词汇
scrutiny
严格审视、仔细核查。face scrutiny from investors = 面临投资者更严苛的拷问。财报季高频词。
13语法
translate into
转化为、体现为。gains translate into growth = 涨幅能否变成真增长。商业英语固定搭配。
12词汇
frenzy
狂热、躁动。the AI frenzy = AI 热潮。带一点“过热”色彩，比 boom / boomlet 更口语夸张。
[ … ]

Stocks are reaching for records14 often because of the tech sector, but remain very volatile on a daily basis15, partly because of big shifts in oil prices and their impact on bond yields and inflation.

15词汇
volatile / on a daily basis
波动剧烈的；按日计。on a daily basis = 日度层面。写“日线晃、趋势仍上”常用。
14地道表达
reach for records
一再冲击纪录高位。reach for = 伸手去够，喻股价不断试探新高。和 ran toward records 同族。

The price of Brent crude, the international standard, rose 1.8% to settle at $106.6516 per barrel. Prices have been swinging between $96 and nearly $11017 over the last month over uncertainty about the U.S. war with Iran and its continued impact on global energy supplies and inflation.

17语法
swing between A and B
在 A 与 B 之间大幅摆动。写油价/汇率区间震荡：swing / bounce / pinball between。
16地道表达
settle at
收于、定格在。oil settled at… = 结算/收盘价。与 10/11 期 settle at 巩固。

The surge in energy prices and its impact on the broader economy will be a key focus this week18 as Wall Street reviews several important economic updates. They include fresh data on the housing market, inflation and retail sales.

18词汇
key focus
本周关键关注点。be a key focus = 成为市场盯盘核心。也说 top of mind / front and center。

The big updates this week involve reports about inflation hitting consumers and businesses. On Wednesday, the government will release the closely-watched consumer price index19, or CPI, which measures price changes for a wide range of products and services. Economists expect the index to show the rate of inflation worsened to 3.6%20 in September from 3.4% in August.

19词汇
closely-watched
备受关注的。closely-watched CPI / data / vote。和 high-profile 近义，偏“市场盯着看”。
20语法
worsen to
恶化至…。通胀“恶化”用 worsen（升到更高），冷却用 cool / ease。注意主语是 the rate of inflation。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A kicked off … with a mixed finish, although B remained … A 以涨跌互现收官开局，尽管 B 仍…
    原文：Wall Street kicked off a busy week on Monday with a mixed finish for the major U.S. stock indexes, although the market remained near its all-time high.
    The team kicked off the season with a mixed finish, although morale remained high.
    球队以胜负参半开季，尽管士气仍高。
  2. Despite those gains, a slide in A dragged B into the red 尽管有那些涨幅，A 的下滑把 B 拖进下跌
    原文：Despite those gains, a slide in big technology stocks dragged the market into the red.
    Despite those gains, a slide in cloud stocks dragged the Nasdaq into the red.
    尽管有那些涨幅，云股下滑把纳指拖进下跌。
  3. whether A can translate into B A 能否转化为 B
    原文：…whether the stock gains made during the frenzy over artificial intelligence can translate into future growth.
    Investors asked whether the user growth can translate into higher profits.
    投资者追问用户增长能否转化为更高利润。
  4. A will be a key focus … as B reviews C 在 B 审视 C 之际，A 将成为关键焦点
    原文：The surge in energy prices and its impact on the broader economy will be a key focus this week as Wall Street reviews several important economic updates.
    Jobless claims will be a key focus this week as traders review the Fed minutes.
    在交易员研读美联储纪要之际，初请失业金将成为本周关键焦点。
