US markets are weighed down by an extended sell-off in the tech sector

科技股抛售延续：标普周跌约 2%，纳指录得 6 月以来最差一周

By Alex Veiga · The Associated Press · 2026-10-16 16:38 EDT（北京时间 10 月 17 日 04:38；WRAL 页更新时间）

今天读美联社（AP）周五收盘综述。芯片股领跌（美光、AMD、博通、英特尔跌 4.5%–6%），标普周跌约 2%、抹去上周创纪录的涨幅，纳指录得 6 月以来最差一周；十年期美债收益率 5.09%，12 月加息概率升至 74.3%。重点看：cap off / wipe out / hold up / all but / in the red / investment spree 这类财经套话。

A broad sell-off1 in technology companies pulled stocks lower2 on Wall Street Friday, capping off3 the market’s worst week since late June.

1词汇
sell-off
/ˈselɔːf/ n. 抛售潮、集中卖出。a broad sell-off = 普遍抛售。例：A sell-off in chip stocks dragged the Nasdaq lower.
3地道表达
cap off
为……收尾、画上句号（多指一段时期的结尾）。capping off the market’s worst week = 为最差一周收官。也可 cap a week / round off。
2地道表达
pull … lower
拖累……下跌。pulled stocks lower = 把股市拉低。反义 push / lift stocks higher。同义 drag down / weigh on。

The S&P 500 fell 1.1%. Its roughly 2% drop for the week wiped out its gains4 from last week, when it set an all-time high5.

5语法
非限制性 when 从句
when 引导从句修饰前面的 last week：「上周——那时它创下历史新高」。逗号后的 when = and at that time，补充说明时间背景。
4地道表达
wipe out gains
抹去涨幅、把涨的全跌回去。wiped out its gains from last week = 把上周涨幅全部吐回。同义 erase / give back gains。

The Nasdaq composite, which is heavily weighted with6 technology stocks, sank 2.1% and posted its first weekly loss in five weeks7 and its worst since June.

7语法
the first … in + 时间段
……以来首次。first weekly loss in five weeks = 五周来首次周跌。in 表「在这段时间内」，类似 the first rate hike in three years。
6地道表达
be weighted with / toward
（指数）权重偏重于……。heavily weighted with technology stocks = 科技股占比很大。也说 tech-heavy。

The Dow Jones Industrial Average held up better8, closing 0.3% lower.

8地道表达
hold up
顶住、抗跌。held up better = 表现相对坚挺。原文后面 Energy stocks held up better than most 同理。

Slightly more than half of the stocks in the S&P 500 fell, but all but seven of the 75 stocks9 in the index’s tech sector closed lower. Among the biggest decliners10 were chip companies, reflecting mounting worries about11 how long the frenzy over the AI buildout12 will last.

9语法
all but + 数量
除了……之外全部。all but seven of the 75 stocks … closed lower = 75 只里只有 7 只没跌。注意 all but 也可作「几乎」：all but certain = 几乎肯定。
11语法
现在分词短语作补充说明
reflecting … 是现在分词短语，补充说明前句「芯片股领跌」意味着什么：反映出对……的担忧加剧。后接 how long … will last 是 about 的宾语从句，用陈述语序。
10词汇
decliner
/dɪˈklaɪnər/ n. 下跌股。biggest decliners = 跌幅最大的股票。对应 gainers / advancers（上涨股）。例：Chipmakers were among the biggest decliners.
12词汇
buildout
/ˈbɪldaʊt/ n. （大规模）建设、扩建。the AI buildout = AI 基础设施（数据中心、算力）大建设。例：The AI buildout requires huge amounts of power.

Micron Technology fell 5.6%, Advanced Micro Devices dropped 6%, Broadcom lost 4.5% and Intel gave up 5.8%13.

13地道表达
give up + 百分比
（股价）回落、跌去。Intel gave up 5.8% = 英特尔跌 5.8%。同段换着说：fell / dropped / lost / gave up，收盘稿避免重复的典型写法。

Among the other tech companies that finished in the red14 were Meta, which fell 3.7%, and Google parent Alphabet, which dropped 2.5%.

14地道表达
in the red
收跌、处于亏损（红字记账）。finished in the red = 收盘下跌。反义 in the green / in positive territory。注意美股红跌绿涨，和 A 股相反。

It’s been a choppy week15 for chipmakers and other big tech stocks central to the artificial intelligence industry16. Investors are now worried that the companies, which have relied on debt markets to finance17 an investment spree18 into their technology, may not be able to keep borrowing and spending as heavily with yields in bond markets worldwide recently climbing19 to their highest levels in years.

15词汇
choppy
/ˈtʃɑːpi/ adj. （行情）起伏不定的、震荡的。本义「波浪起伏的海面」。例：Stocks had a choppy session, swinging between gains and losses.
17语法
长句拆解
主干：Investors are now worried that the companies … may not be able to keep borrowing and spending。which 从句插在主谓之间，说明这些公司靠债市融资；to finance … 是目的状语。
19语法
with 复合结构表原因
with + 名词 + 现在分词：「因为全球债市收益率最近攀升」。逻辑主语 yields 与 climbing 是主动关系，整个结构说明「为何借不动」。
16语法
形容词短语后置作定语
central to … 修饰前面的 big tech stocks，相当于 that are central to …：「对 AI 产业至关重要的大型科技股」。形容词带补语时要放名词后面。
18词汇
spree
/spriː/ n. 一阵狂热的（消费/投资）活动。investment spree = 投资狂潮；shopping / spending spree = 疯狂购物/花钱。例：The company went on a hiring spree.

In the bond market, Treasury yields held relatively steady20. The yield on the 10-year Treasury slipped to 5.09% from 5.10%21 late Thursday. It had topped 5.30%22 earlier this month and touched its highest level since 2002 because of worries about high inflation, signals of a strong U.S. economy and other factors.

21语法
slip to X from Y
从 Y 小幅降至 X。注意新数在前、旧数在后：to 新值 from 旧值。slip 比 fall 幅度更小。
20地道表达
hold steady
保持平稳、基本不动。Treasury yields held relatively steady = 美债收益率相对平稳。同义 be little changed / hover。
22语法
过去完成时 had done
had topped 表示在「周四/本周」之前更早发生：本月早些时候曾突破 5.30%。top 作动词 = 超过、突破。

Yields are down despite reports this week showing23 that inflation at the consumer and wholesale level24 rose more in the U.S. than economists expected.

23语法
despite + 名词 + 分词
despite 是介词，后接名词 reports，showing … 修饰 reports（= reports that show …）。不能写 despite of。同义 in spite of / even though + 从句。
24词汇
wholesale
/ˈhoʊlseɪl/ adj./n. 批发（的）。the consumer and wholesale level = 消费端（CPI）和批发端（PPI，生产者物价）。例：Wholesale prices rose 0.5% last month.

The reports could persuade the inflation-fighters25 at the Federal Reserve to hike their key interest rate26 before the end of this year. The central bank increased its key interest rate by a quarter-point27 to about 3.9% in September, its first increase in three years.

25词汇
inflation-fighter
/ɪnˈfleɪʃən ˌfaɪtər/ n. 抗通胀者。AP 常用来指美联储官员。例：The Fed’s inflation-fighters may raise rates again.
27词汇
quarter-point
/ˈkwɔːrtər pɔɪnt/ n. 0.25 个百分点（25 个基点）。increased … by a quarter-point = 加息 25 个基点。by 表变化幅度。
26词汇
hike
/haɪk/ v./n. 上调（利率、价格）。hike the rate = 加息；a rate hike = 一次加息。反义 cut。例：The Fed may hike rates in December.

Wall Street traders expect the Fed will keep its short-term rate unchanged28 at its policy meeting this month, but the odds of a rate increase29 at the December meeting have jumped to 74.3% from 50.2% a month ago, according to data from the CME Group30.

29地道表达
the odds of …
……的概率/可能性。the odds of a rate increase = 加息概率。odds 恒用复数；odds are that … = 很可能……。
28地道表达
keep … unchanged
维持……不变（按兵不动）。keep rates unchanged = 利率不变。同义 hold rates steady / leave rates on hold。
30词汇
CME Group
芝加哥商品交易所集团。其 FedWatch 工具根据联邦基金期货价格推算市场对美联储加息/降息的概率预期。
[ … ]

All told,31 the S&P 500 fell 84.62 points to 7,659.44. The Dow dropped 175.28 points to 52,002.41, and the Nasdaq declined 572.99 points to close at 26,140.03.

31地道表达
all told
总计、合计下来。收盘稿固定收尾：All told, the S&P 500 fell … points to …。10/15 期出现过，巩固一下。

可以模仿的句型

  1. A pulled B lower …, capping off C A 拖累 B 下跌，为 C 画上句号
    原文：A broad sell-off in technology companies pulled stocks lower on Wall Street Friday, capping off the market’s worst week since late June.
    A slump in bank shares pulled European stocks lower on Thursday, capping off their worst month since March.
    银行股下挫拖累欧股周四走低，为其 3 月以来最差的一个月收尾。
  2. all but X of the Y … closed lower Y 个……里除了 X 个以外全部收跌
    原文：Slightly more than half of the stocks in the S&P 500 fell, but all but seven of the 75 stocks in the index’s tech sector closed lower.
    All but two of the 11 sectors in the S&P 500 closed higher after the jobs report.
    就业报告公布后，标普 500 的 11 个板块中除两个外全部收涨。
  3. the odds of A have jumped to X from Y …, according to B 据 B，A 的概率已从 Y 升至 X
    原文：…the odds of a rate increase at the December meeting have jumped to 74.3% from 50.2% a month ago, according to data from the CME Group.
    The odds of a recession next year have jumped to 40% from 25% in July, according to a survey of economists.
    据一项经济学家调查，明年陷入衰退的概率已从 7 月的 25% 升至 40%。
